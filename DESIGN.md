## The game in one paragraph

You are a fixer in **Lantern Bay** (燈籠灣, Dang Lung Wan), a fictional Hong Kong-inspired megacity, in 2057. You start by picking three people from six who'll work for street wages, with one safehouse and rent due on Sunday. Over the years you build them into a syndicate. There is no ending: like Football Manager, the story comes from the people you recruit, keep alive, lose, and remember.

## Setting

- **City:** fictional, Hong Kong-inspired, multicultural megacity. Cantonese Chinese culture is the base layer (street life, superstitions, mahjong parlours, night markets, master-and-apprentice traditions), with large Filipino, South Asian, African and European communities layered on top, as in a real port megacity.
- **Districts:** Old Town, the Docks, the Night Market and Glass Heights are open from the start; Iron Peak, the Silt and the Amber Reach are lore for now and unlock later. See `WORLD.md`.
- **Cast mix:** leans Hong Kong Chinese (about 3 in 5) with a multicultural mix. Applies to the starting crew and, later, to generated recruits.

## World

The lore bible (history, power map, districts, Pearl Haven House) and the context block for worldbuilding prompts live in `WORLD.md`. Decisions about it are still logged below.

## Tone

**Gritty but human** (working assumption; confirm after the first playtest). Losses hurt, but the baseline is warmth, loyalty and dark humour. Darker moments arrive as events, not as the default mood.

## Open questions

- **Does heat earn its place? (updated 2026-10-24)** Heat now comes only from Loud jobs and crisis costs, cools 2 a week, and at 6+ brings one fixer notice (pay ¥1,500 for −3, or let it ride for −1 on the next job's first crisis roll). Careful mixed play barely builds any; all-Loud play meets the fixer most months. Watch the playtests: if nobody thinks about heat, cut it rather than adding more consequences. Earlier note, kept for history: **Heat does nothing yet.** Cooling was added (see log), but high heat has no consequences. Candidates: police attention events, harder crises, safehouse raids, clients refusing to hire you.
- **A bigger crew needs more work.** Recruiting and expansion now exist, but the board still offers at most 4 jobs a week, one crew at a time. Growing past about 8 needs multiple simultaneous jobs, then delegation to captains (the Act II/III plan).
