# Code map

Where things live and how a run flows, so you can read a few functions instead of whole files. Rules (architecture, tests, docs, commits) are in `CLAUDE.md`; status and what's next in `HANDOFF.md`; why each choice was made in `DESIGN.md` (decision log rows in `docs/design/`). Keep this file short and current: when you move something, fix its line here. **One file per line under "Core files" and "UI files": grep the file name (`grep -n "heat.js" CODEMAP.md`) instead of reading the whole map.**

How battles work (both engines, every number, the death rule): `docs/BATTLES.md`. See also `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (state shapes, economy loop, `finish()`, saves, tests). For the other tongs (rivals, free names, scouting, buyouts, rival offers) start at `docs/RIVAL-TONGS.md`.

## Layers
- The layer rules (core pure, `src/ui/` the only DOM, `save.js` the only storage, content as data) are in `CLAUDE.md` "Architecture".
- Every roll is `d20 + stat >= 10 + difficulty` (`core/risk.js` `successChance`). Randomness: `nextRng(state)` (`core/random.js`, moves the state's counter) or `makeRng(seed)` (`core/rng.js`, doesn't). Never `Math.random()` in core.

## The run, in order
`startMission` builds `am` (the active mission, `state.activeMission`): a **queue** of steps, `beat | zone | incident | crisis | battle`, and `am.step` points at the next one. One function per step type, all returning a new state:

| Step | Function | File |
| --- | --- | --- |
| beat | `revealBeat` | `core/mission.js` |
| zone | `enterZone` | `core/mission.js` |
| incident | `runIncident` (roll, save, cost; an unsaved danger incident starts a fight) | `core/mission.js` |
| battle | `runBattle` (one round, then `afterRound`); a loud run's first guard always starts one (`loudFightDue`, `core/battle.js`) | `core/runbattle.js` |
| crisis | `describeCrisis` / `chooseOption` (the player's or the orders' choice) | `core/mission.js` |
| critical | `describeCritical` / `resolveCritical` (stabilise, extract, push on) | `core/mission.js` |
| call-in | `describePending` / `resolvePending` (`am.pending`: `alert`, `injury`, `door`, `standoff`) | `core/mission.js` |
| end | `finish` (pay, heat, reputation, growth, report), then `closeMission` | `core/mission.js` |

`core/autorun.js` `autoStep` takes the next step the crew can take on its own; `needsPlayer` says when to stop for a call-in; `autoRun` plays a whole hands-off job. `core/mission.js` sections are marked `// --- name ---`; `grep -n "^// ---"` finds them.

**Away jobs** (`core/away.js`, 2026-11-04): a crew sent away plays a share a day. `advanceAway` (the day tick, from `time.js`) steps each away job to today's stopping beat (`beatsByDay`, `dayDone` in `autorun.js`, which `needsPlayer` reads); one that needs the player waits (`waiting`) with a blocking `kind: 'callin'` message. `takeCall` puts it in `state.activeMission` (the watch screen); `letThemRun` hands it back at the day's end; `takeCommand` plays the rest now. Away crews fight on their own unless under command. `crewsOut` (with `phaseOf`, status and beat progress) feeds Today's crew-out cards; reports carry `back` for Today's back cards; quiet-day lines in `data/away.js`. Backlog for the command centre and presence: `drafts/ops-and-presence.md`.

## The watch screen
`ui/watch.js` runs the feed on a timer and hands each new card's `cue` to `ui/siteplan.js` (which draws the site with `ui/siteart.js`: shadows, light, stalls and crowd). A fight waits for orders each round (`needsPlayer`): for engine 1 `ui/fightorders.js` draws the orders panel in the call-in slot and the map's overlay (`plan.command`); for engine 2 (the five jobs) `ui/fightscreen.js` draws the round screen and the call-in cards there (health as one bar, `hpLine`, 2026-11-19; an enemy card's statuses: `statusRows`, 2026-11-19), and its view comes from `core/fightview.js` (`statusesView`: each status an enemy carries, a label and the line a tap opens; `roundView`, `planView`: only the rules' read models, no chance of its own; `buildRead`, 2026-11-17: Joss's build advice and its breakdown for the plan screen, drawn closed by `fightscreen.js buildAdvice`, `V.fightAdviceOpen`). **The live battle view (2026-11-12):** an engine-2 fight plays on the site map itself. Each battle event carries `stage` (`core/runfight.js stageOf`: everyone's place from `core/fightmap.js layoutFight`, compact) and the director's `beats`; `feed.js` passes them in `cue.battle`, and `siteplan.js` hands a cue with a stage to `ui/fightstage.js` (`stageScript`, pure: a round as steps, re-stance, the exchange in the rules' order, the settle; `createStage`, the player: circular faces for both sides, pips, low barriers for `mark`, bodies, tracers). Pace (`fightscreen.js fightPace`): Fast or Watch is `getPref('fight-watch')` (chips on the round screen, `fs-pace` in `actions.js`); Resolve is `fs-handoff`. Fast is `watch.js fastFight`: once the round's picture is done, the next round plays on the orders as they stand (`onFightNext`, passed to `mountWatch` by `app.js render`), never while the orders are open or a call-in waits. Step 3: the stage's `say` steps feed `watch.js commentary` (the strip under the map and the folded log, `clockText`), `camera` steps push in on a spotlight or follow a special move, and `ambientTick` (pure, seeded) shuffles the standing on their spots and fires wide between rounds. Step 4 (2026-11-14): a run of `burst` lines is a special move, drawn in the star's look (`stage.units[id].look`, `core/fightmap.js specialLook`; looks, timings and swarm knots in `data/fightLooks.js`): `special` (letterbox bars on `.watch__map`, the title), `wave` (ping), `fire` steps with `look` (ricochet, slash, ping), `specialEnd`; `forceSpecial(cue, look)` forces one (tests, the preview). Knots (`knotToken`) are for a planned swarm-drone kind only. Preview in a browser: `node scripts/preview-livebattle.mjs <dir>` (`--special ricochet|slash|ping`, `--knots`, `--foes`, `--crew`, `--throttle`, `--scale`). `core/runfight.js` draws a watched fight's next round as soon as the last one ends (`drawAhead`), so the threats show before orders. App.js plays the round with `runBattle(state, orders)`, `handOffFight` or `pullBack`; the plan screen's fight block (`renderFightPlan`) reads `coverage().fight2`. Guards still ahead wait on their posts: `guardPosts(am)` (`core/battle.js`) → `plan.posts()`, at each site's `posts` spots. `core/feed.js` builds the cards and cues from `am.events`.

## Site maps
`data/sites.js`: plain sites, and districts drawn as parts (`PLOTS`: a plot with approaches, covers and targets; a target with `jobs` is that job's own place). `pickSite(district, jobId, templateId)` picks the parts from the job's id (stable, nothing stored) and `assemble()` makes them a site. The core reads a site only for fights: `core/fightspots.js` `fightSpots(site, zone)` puts both sides on spots behind the drawn cover (every district: `data/battle.js` `spotsIn`); `node scripts/preview-fightspots.mjs <district> <png>` draws them, `tests/fightspots.test.js` checks them. `ui/siteplan.js` draws it. `node scripts/preview-sites.mjs <district>` draws every combination; `tests/sites.test.js` checks each (geometry, routes clear of cover, a job's own target).

## Events and the record
Everything that happens in a run is `record(am, event)` into `am.events`. `feed.js` turns events into watch cards; `report.js` and the report's full log (`ui/messages.js` `renderFullLog`) read the same events, as the report stores them (`report.js reportEvent` drops a fight's staging and keeps its lines lean, so a field the log draws must be in its `LINE_KEEPS`). A new kind of moment needs its event, a card in `feed.js` and a line in `renderFullLog`.
