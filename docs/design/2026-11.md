| 2026-11-03 | **New jobs can be drafted outside a session with a ready prompt, `docs/new-job-prompt.md`, that returns the game's own file format** (owner: "generate a prompt so I can generate more missions outside without your token usage"). It carries WORLD.md's context block word for word (a test, `tests/prompts.test.js`, fails if they drift), the rules `core/validate.js` enforces, difficulty and pay by tier from the existing jobs, the text tokens `fill()` knows (Fung has none), and a cut-down real job (Wrong Horse) as the template. Adding a pasted job is then a file, one import, a client's job list and a test run. WORLD.md's context block now says the crew starts as three founders picked from six (it still said five). | Converting plain-English pitches (WRITING_GUIDE.md) costs a session the most tokens; a prompt that writes the data directly moves that work to any chat, and the validator and tests still catch mistakes when it's added. | Plain-English templates only: the conversion is the expensive part. A JSON format: the jobs are JS modules with comments, and one shape everywhere keeps adding a job trivial. |
| 2026-11-03 | **Runner record (renown slice 1).** `op.record` (`core/record.js`) is written once per finished job in `finish()`, for everyone who took part (inside, overwatch, wheels, standby; hands-off runs too; the dead go to the memorial with a short summary; freelancers get none). It counts only: no roll, pay, heat or risk changes. Created lazily like `earned`/`track`; save v30 seeds old crews from `jobsWithCrew`, marked `estimated`. "Clean" = completed, nobody went loud, no heat; "flawless" = completed and nobody went critical; "carry" = a successful crisis roll at difficulty 5+ on a job at the runner's tier level (tier level: rookie 1, pro 2, veteran 3, job threat at least that). Crisis roll events now carry `difficulty`. Enemies defeated aren't tracked per runner (battle events only say who stood in the fight), so fights won stands in for it; the Enforcer path leans on fights won and combat carries. Named `record`, not `career`, to avoid the stat "career +n" text and career marks. | The first playtest wanted attachment to the crew. A record gives every name a history before any fame, standing or retirement exists, and costs no balance. Proposal: drafts/PROPOSAL-runner-renown.md (owner's design; Opus/Sonnet review 2026-11-03). | Storing a record on every operative at creation (lazy is enough); counting kills with a guessed split (a made-up number would mislead); a separate renown-points score (the record is the one source of truth). |
| 2026-11-03 | **Walking away from a decision pays nothing.** `exitRun` (`core/mission.js`) now sets the job's pay cut to 100%, so every decision exit (alert "Get out", hurt "Get out", death's door, standoff, "Nobody left who can carry on") pays ¥0, as the button says: "Everyone comes home. No pay." It takes an optional `payCut` for any exit that should pay part. Heat, reputation and trust are unchanged. Early-game sim (60 seeds, careful play, medians): three founders without hiring, week 4 ¥11,500 to ¥11,000, week 13 ¥18,100 to ¥16,800, week 26 ¥34,250 to ¥31,700; three founders hiring up to five, week 26 ¥15,150 to ¥9,800 and broke 15% to 22%; five founders, week 26 ¥15,750 to ¥12,550. Reputation and deaths did not move. The economy test (careful, 4 weeks) still passes. | The button promised "No pay" and the code paid the full fee: a free, safe payday every time the alarm went up. Fights already took the pay (`retreat` sets the cut to 100%), so the two kinds of exit now agree. | Change the button text to "Full pay" (makes bailing the best move). Pay a share, e.g. 50% like extracting a wounded operative (the buttons say no pay, and a half fee still rewards quitting early). |
