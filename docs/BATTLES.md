## Engine 2 counts in HP (2026-11-18)

Engine 2's health, hits, heals, ticks and pull-out points are in HP. Step 1 of the move from pips multiplied every amount by `FIGHT.hpUnit` (10), so the rules play exactly as before: where this file or an older note says a pip, read 10 HP. A plate still takes one plain hit (`FIGHT.plateHp`, 10). Pull-out points round up to a whole `hpUnit`. The screens still draw one bar segment per 10 HP (`fight.js hpUnits`, read by `fightview.js` and `runfight.js fightSnapshot`/`feedLine`). Engine 1 keeps its own HP. Save v77 converts a fight in progress.

## Hit size and variance (2026-11-18, save v78)

A weapon's damage sets what its hit takes off: `fight.js hitOf(damage)` = `FIGHT.hit` (10) + `hitPerDamage` (3) for each point above `hitBaseDamage` (2, unarmed), so 10 unarmed, 13 for the default sidearm, up to 28 at damage 8. A runner carries it as `r.hit` (from `runnerOf`); overwatch and the hall turret work it out from their own `damage`. A strong hit is `strongMult` (2) times the hit, on one margin for every weapon (`FIGHT.strongMargin`, 12). `landHit` lands a hit on an enemy: a plate (unless the hit pierces) takes `plateHp` (10) off one hit and the rest goes through, one plate a hit however big; then whatever got through wobbles by `hitSpreadShare` (0.15) of the hit's size, rounded (±2 at 10, ±3 at 20), never below 1. An enemy's shot wobbles the same way when it is drawn, so the shown hit is the used one. The planning rule that skips an enemy already covered (`covered`) adds up the planned hits' sizes the same way. Enemy shots and a runner's armour are as before. Why: `drafts/PROPOSAL-gear-hit-size.md`.

## The build vocabulary's first statuses (2026-11-19, save v81)

Part B of `drafts/HANDOFF-build-vocab-next.md`, on the owner's answers: four new statuses beside `infected`, the trigger `onCrack` and the condition `targetHas`, all in `fight.js VOCAB`. Each status is a field of its name on an enemy (null when it has none) made by `STATUS[st]`; `put` lays one on (never on a body that has it, never on an immune one) with its own line (`STATUS_LINE`). What each does by itself:

| Status | Does | Where in `fight.js` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `burning` | Loses the payload's `dot.tick` HP at each round's end from the round after, for `dot.rounds`. A plain burn: no piece spreads it, and a body it drops passes nothing on (owner). | `ticksDue` (now `[{ e, st }]`, infection first), the tick loop (`burnTick`/`burnOut`) |
| `marked` | Nothing. A mark other pieces read with `if: { targetHas: 'marked' }`. | `pieceHolds(b, cond, e)` |
| `exposed` | The next hit it takes from the crew or the overwatch ignores its plate (the plate stays for the shot after), deals the payload's `expose.extra` (10) HP more (owner, 2026-11-19), and uses the status up; the hit's line carries `pierced`. | `pierce`, `extraOf` in `playRound` |
| `shaken` | Counts as gone in its group's flee check, so a group breaks sooner. Only kinds that break run, so it does nothing to corp, enforcers or drones. | the flee check (`holding`) |

`onCrack` fires on an attack or a bounce that only cracks a plate (no damage): its `apply` pieces go on whatever the plate (the onHit ones still need damage past it). A killer's `onKill` piece can now `apply` as well as `spread` (Rout); a status a body carries still brings only its payload's `spread` pieces when it falls, never its other onKill pieces. `nextInGroup` passes over a body the status would be wasted on (`canAct`: an exposed body needs a plate left, a shaken one must be a kind that breaks).

Four payloads use them (`data/builds.js`; offered by Joss like the others since the owner's call the same day, under Claude's draft names; `core/builds.js offerPick` shows three of those a runner qualifies for, seeded by the runner and the day; a piece marked `draft: true` would be neither offered nor listed as reachable): **Incendiary Rounds** (onHit and onCrack: `burning`, 10 HP for 2 rounds, machines burn), **Spotter's Eye** (onHit and onCrack: `marked`), **Breacher** (onCrack, or onHit on a marked body: the next of the group `exposed`, +10 HP on its next hit), **Rout** (onKill: the next of the group `shaken`; a marked kill shakes one more). All four take the payload slot, so Spotter's Eye beside Breacher or Rout is a two-runner job. Drones are `armoured` as well as `synthetic` (owner); police and secforce are `armoured`; the build read's armoured line reads the tag. Every status is in `oddsKey`, the preview's `ticks` carry `status` and `word`, the snapshot carries `st` and the stage draws each its own look (`ui/fightstage.js statuses`: an orange flickering ring, a red crosshair, a yellow broken plate, a jittering violet ring; `forceEffect(cue, 'statuses')` for the preview script). A fight saved before v81 gives its enemies none. Tests: `tests/statuses.test.js`. Sweep: `docs/sims/combos-2026-11-19-statuses.md` (before: `-statuses-before.md`; with the exposed +10 HP: `-statuses-offered.md`).

## The build vocabulary's trigger step (2026-11-19)

What a move or payload does is data: `data/builds.js` gives each a `pieces` list, each piece `{ when, if?, who, what }` in the words `fight.js VOCAB` knows (when `onAttack`/`onHit`/`onKill`; if `enemiesUp`; who `target`/`nextInGroup`/`nearest`; what `burst`/`apply`/`spread`; the status `infected`). A piece never names another piece. `piecesOn(r, when)` lists a runner's listeners in a fixed order (the move's, then the payload's). Ricochet is an `onAttack` piece (`moveReady`, its `if` read by `pieceHolds`; `moveNeedsUp` for the planning advice); the Vectors are `onHit` `apply` pieces (`landed` in `playRound`) and Contagion adds an `onKill` `spread` piece (`fall`). A status on a body brings its payload's `onKill` pieces with it, so whatever drops an infected host can make it jump. The guardrails live in the engine, not the pieces: a status never stacks or renews, an immune body shrugs once and never passes one on, a fall spreads a status at most once (the killer's own piece first). Built as a refactor: 2,592 fights over every build mix played line-for-line the same before and after, at the same speed. Adding a piece made of these words needs no engine code (`tests/vocab.test.js`). Why: `drafts/PROPOSAL-build-vocabulary.md`.

## Armour as a flat soak (2026-11-19, save v80)

A runner's armour takes `FIGHT.soakPerArmour` (5) HP per armour point off every enemy shot that lands, and at least `soakMin` (1) always gets through: `fight.js soakOf(value)` on the runner as `r.soak` (from `runnerOf`, the vest plus a skin weave), applied by `landsOn(v, hit)` in `playRound`'s shot. A value-1 vest takes 5 off a 10 HP or 20 HP shot, value 2 takes 10, value 3 takes 15. Runners have no plates any more (`r.absorb` 0); enemies keep theirs. The shown hit is the used one: `exposures` carry `lands` (the HP after the soak of whoever faces the shot, a shield included), which the threat line and the runner's shots on the round screen show; the shot ranking the defaults use (`shotValue`) and `applyPatch`'s "exposed" read it too. A landed shot's line carries `soaked`. `r.soak` is in `oddsKey`. The armour card shows it (`view.soak`, Claude's draft). A fight saved before v80 keeps its plates (`soak` 0). Why 5, not the 3 first agreed: at 3 the soak was worth well under the old plates (a fight lands only a few shots on each runner, and a plate stopped a whole one); `docs/sims/armour-soak-2026-11-19.md`. Engine 1 keeps its own armour (1 off every hit in its units).

## Enemy toughness (2026-11-18, save v79)

A job's threat makes every body in its fight tougher: `data/fights.js TOUGHNESS_BY_THREAT` gives the steps (threat 1-3: 0, threats 4 and 5: 1; threat 5 was 2, +10 HP, until the owner halved it on 2026-11-19), each step `TOUGHNESS.hpStep` (5) more HP, so threat 4 and 5 bodies have +5 HP (a hired grunt 10 or 15; a corp 20 or 25). `buildFight` stores the step as `b.toughness` and `newEnemy` applies it, so arrivals (backup, pressure, waves, the way out) are as tough as the first bodies. From `TOUGHNESS.plateAtStep` (3) steps a body also gets one more plate; no threat reaches it yet (the first sim found HP and a plate together far too sharp, `docs/sims/enemy-toughness-2026-11-18-step1.md`), so it waits for the week or standing steps. Raids on a hall are not tougher. Enemy shots and runner armour are as before (armour as a flat soak is step 2). `b.toughness` (and each enemy's `max` and `absorb`) are in `oddsKey`. A fight saved before has toughness 0. Why: `drafts/PROPOSAL-enemy-toughness.md`.

## The way out of a hack (2026-11-17)

A hack fight (type `holdHack`) has two stages. Stage 0 is the hack: the technician works `FIGHT.workNeeded` (4) rounds, with the deadline of 7 rounds, and everyone else covers them. When the work is done the alarm brings in half the opening force's count again (`b.wayOut`, read when the fight is built, `FIGHT.wayOutShare` 0.5 rounded up; they arrive together next round as one group) and the fight goes to stage 1, the way out. In stage 1 everyone attacks (the work is finished, so the technician is one of the crew), there is no deadline, and the fight is won only when every enemy is down or fled and none is on the way (`end.wayOutWon`), as the yard is. The alarm clock can still bring a few reinforcements (`maxExtraEnemies`). Losing the technician or the technician pulling out ends the fight only in stage 0. Breaking off in stage 1 (someone down on cautious or steady, or "pull back" at death's door) keeps the objective and the pay (`objectiveDone`, `end.wayOutPulled`) at the cost of whoever is down; the planning odds count it as a win. The medic's hands (`treatWayOut` in `checkEnd`): before a way-out ends, the best healer still standing rolls to stabilise each downed runner (`FIGHT.wayOutTreatStep`) and gives each hurt runner who is up `wayOutTreatHeal` pips; a runner so steadied skips the serious injury and the death's-door prompt. A fight saved before this has no `wayOut` and still ends with the hack.
