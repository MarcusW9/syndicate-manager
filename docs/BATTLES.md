## Engine 2 counts in HP (2026-11-18)

Engine 2's health, hits, heals, ticks and pull-out points are in HP. Step 1 of the move from pips multiplied every amount by `FIGHT.hpUnit` (10), so the rules play exactly as before: where this file or an older note says a pip, read 10 HP. A plate still takes one plain hit (`FIGHT.plateHp`, 10). Pull-out points round up to a whole `hpUnit`. The screens still draw one bar segment per 10 HP (`fight.js hpUnits`, read by `fightview.js` and `runfight.js fightSnapshot`/`feedLine`). Engine 1 keeps its own HP. Save v77 converts a fight in progress.

## Hit size and variance (2026-11-18, save v78)

A weapon's damage sets what its hit takes off: `fight.js hitOf(damage)` = `FIGHT.hit` (10) + `hitPerDamage` (3) for each point above `hitBaseDamage` (2, unarmed), so 10 unarmed, 13 for the default sidearm, up to 28 at damage 8. A runner carries it as `r.hit` (from `runnerOf`); overwatch and the hall turret work it out from their own `damage`. A strong hit is `strongMult` (2) times the hit, on one margin for every weapon (`FIGHT.strongMargin`, 12). `landHit` lands a hit on an enemy: a plate (unless the hit pierces) takes `plateHp` (10) off one hit and the rest goes through, one plate a hit however big; then whatever got through wobbles by `hitSpreadShare` (0.15) of the hit's size, rounded (±2 at 10, ±3 at 20), never below 1. An enemy's shot wobbles the same way when it is drawn, so the shown hit is the used one. The planning rule that skips an enemy already covered (`covered`) adds up the planned hits' sizes the same way. Enemy shots and a runner's armour are as before. Why: `drafts/PROPOSAL-gear-hit-size.md`.

## Enemy toughness (2026-11-18, save v79)

A job's threat makes every body in its fight tougher: `data/fights.js TOUGHNESS_BY_THREAT` gives the steps (threat 1-3: 0, threat 4: 1, threat 5: 2), each step `TOUGHNESS.hpStep` (5) more HP, so threat 4 bodies have +5 HP and threat 5 +10 (a hired grunt 10, 15, 20; a corp 20, 25, 30). `buildFight` stores the step as `b.toughness` and `newEnemy` applies it, so arrivals (backup, pressure, waves, the way out) are as tough as the first bodies. From `TOUGHNESS.plateAtStep` (3) steps a body also gets one more plate; no threat reaches it yet (the first sim found HP and a plate together far too sharp, `docs/sims/enemy-toughness-2026-11-18-step1.md`), so it waits for the week or standing steps. Raids on a hall are not tougher. Enemy shots and runner armour are as before (armour as a flat soak is step 2). `b.toughness` (and each enemy's `max` and `absorb`) are in `oddsKey`. A fight saved before has toughness 0. Why: `drafts/PROPOSAL-enemy-toughness.md`.

## The way out of a hack (2026-11-17)

A hack fight (type `holdHack`) has two stages. Stage 0 is the hack: the technician works `FIGHT.workNeeded` (4) rounds, with the deadline of 7 rounds, and everyone else covers them. When the work is done the alarm brings in half the opening force's count again (`b.wayOut`, read when the fight is built, `FIGHT.wayOutShare` 0.5 rounded up; they arrive together next round as one group) and the fight goes to stage 1, the way out. In stage 1 everyone attacks (the work is finished, so the technician is one of the crew), there is no deadline, and the fight is won only when every enemy is down or fled and none is on the way (`end.wayOutWon`), as the yard is. The alarm clock can still bring a few reinforcements (`maxExtraEnemies`). Losing the technician or the technician pulling out ends the fight only in stage 0. Breaking off in stage 1 (someone down on cautious or steady, or "pull back" at death's door) keeps the objective and the pay (`objectiveDone`, `end.wayOutPulled`) at the cost of whoever is down; the planning odds count it as a win. The medic's hands (`treatWayOut` in `checkEnd`): before a way-out ends, the best healer still standing rolls to stabilise each downed runner (`FIGHT.wayOutTreatStep`) and gives each hurt runner who is up `wayOutTreatHeal` pips; a runner so steadied skips the serious injury and the death's-door prompt. A fight saved before this has no `wayOut` and still ends with the hack.
