# Changelog, 2026-11

Part of the changelog indexed in `CHANGELOG.md`. Newest first; add new entries at the top of the newest file. For *why* a decision was made, see the decision log in `DESIGN.md`.

## 2026-11-18 · The hit is shown
- A sidearm's card now says what its hit takes off: "damage 4 · hits for 16, strong 32". On the fight's round screen each runner has a line, "Hits for 16, strong 32". Both read the numbers the rules use (`hitOf`), so they cannot drift.
- The wording is Claude's draft (`view.hit` in `data/fightText.js`), flagged for the owner in `drafts/writing-tasks.md`. Screenshots: `docs/screenshots/2026-11-18-hit-display`.

## 2026-11-18 · Enemies get tougher with the job's threat (save v79)
- In the new fights every enemy has 5 more HP on a threat-4 job and 10 more on a threat-5 job (a hired grunt 10, 15, 20; a corp 20, 25, 30), reinforcements included. Threat 1-3 jobs are exactly as before, so a new crew's early game does not move. Raids on a hall are unchanged.
- Why: once a crew found guns and vests, threat 5 cost them about what threat 1 did. A crew with a damage-6 weapon and a pro vest still loses almost nothing at threat 5 (about 3 HP); an unarmed crew now loses much more there.
- A fight saved mid-way keeps its old enemies and its later arrivals as they were. The sim and numbers: `docs/sims/enemy-toughness-2026-11-18-step1.md`.

## 2026-11-18 · The owner's wording for builds, the other tongs and the fight screens; no pips left
- New wording for Ricochet and the two Vectors (what each does, and Joss's offer), the build offer's note and its subject when several pieces are offered, Joss's build advice and its breakdown, the Contagion's jump lines, the way out of a hack, the chance of losing someone (now "killed or left behind") and the van call-in.
- The other tongs: how old a read is, what we know of them, the watch trip's messages, what a tong is after, the slate marks (Overrated / Underrated). One line (what a short-table tong is after) kept until it matches the rule.
- The last pip wording is gone: the last-stand spotlight (now on 10 HP or less, since hits vary it had stopped firing), the pull-out "stay in" line, and the Vector's tick line on the round screen, which now says the HP it will take.
- One rule change: the last-stand spotlight fires on 10 HP or less instead of exactly 10.

## 2026-11-18 · A better gun hits harder (engine 2, save v78)
- In the new fights a weapon's damage now sets how much its hit takes off: 10 bare-handed, then 3 more for each point of damage (13 for the default sidearm, 19 for a cannon, up to 28 for the best). A strong hit is still double. Before, every hit was 10 or 20 and a better gun only made the 20 a little more likely, and stopped counting above damage 5.
- Every weapon now lands strong hits equally often (the odds bare hands always had). New crews start unarmed, so their fights are unchanged.
- An enemy's plate takes 10 off one hit and the rest gets through, so a big gun punches through armour; a hit still breaks only one plate. Overwatch and the hall turret use the same rule from their own damage.
- The ±2 wobble on a hit is now about 15% of the hit, so it is still ±2 on a 10 and grows on bigger guns.

## 2026-11-18 · A spare piece instead of scrap
- When a completed job drops no gear, one time in four the crew now gets a Standard sidearm instead of the 50 scrap cash. The report says "Gear from the job: (name). A spare piece." Sidearms now turn up about one completed job in 4, up from one in 13. The drops themselves (odds, tiers, rare pieces) are unchanged.

## 2026-11-18 · Weapon damage measured (no game change)
- A new sim, `scripts/sim-weapon-damage.mjs`, counts the damage on sidearms at each tier, on job drops and on starting crews, for the weapon hit-size proposal. Results in `docs/sims/weapon-damage-2026-11-18.md`.

## 2026-11-18 · Hit variance in engine 2 fights
- A hit that lands, a crew's, an overwatch shot's or an enemy's, now varies by up to 2 HP either way (`FIGHT.hitSpread`): a plain 10 HP hit is 8 to 12. A plate still takes a whole plain hit (0 through). An enemy's damage is rolled when its shot is drawn, so the number shown is the number used. Sim (standard force, warehouse): wins within 2 points, rounds up about 0.2, someone down about 3% to 6%, no deaths.
- Two stage tests assumed their sample had no plate-cracking hits or landed patches; they now allow for both.

## 2026-11-18 · Fights show HP

- On the live map, a hit floats its damage as a number (−20), a heal +10 and an infection's tick −10.

## 2026-11-18 · Fights count in HP (nothing changes yet)
- The new fights now count health, damage, healing and the infection's tick in HP instead of pips: every number was multiplied by 10 (a plain hit 10, a 3-pip runner 30 HP). Fights play exactly as before, checked against hundreds of recorded fights and the vectors and way-out sims, and the screens still show the same segments. It makes room for tougher enemies and finer effects later.
- An old save with a fight in progress converts when it loads (save v77).

## 2026-11-18 · Tattoos drawn properly
- Portrait tattoos were a triangle and two dashes, three marks on the shaved side and a tiny lantern: at small sizes they looked like scribbles. They are now six geometric designs (nested diamond, corner brackets, circuit trace, scale pattern, stepped line, reticle), one per runner fixed by their id, each drawn as dark ink under a neon line with square ends, small and low on the shadow cheek, tilted and faded towards the face's edge and clipped to the head so none can leave the face. The undercut's shaved side has stepped chevrons on a spine and the crew's lantern has a cap, ribs and a tassel (`ui/art.js tattooArt`, `inkPen`; sheet in `docs/screenshots/2026-11-18-tattoos/`).

## 2026-11-18 · A wound no longer looks like a power-up
- On a job map, a runner who took a light injury had a bright sodium-yellow ring, which read as a bonus. It is now a small status badge on the token's top right (`siteplan.js markHurt`): an outlined red cross in a cut-corner square for a light injury, a solid red square with a white cross for a serious one, which also keeps its red ring. A first try, a plaster on the face, covered the face and was dropped.
- A hurt runner's portrait now wears a proper wound instead of the one crude plaster or gauze band: four light looks (plaster, split brow with butterfly strips, black eye, graze), three serious (gauze band, taped cheek pad, split lip) and a critical head wrap, each in the runner's own skin tone and fixed by their id, so the same person always shows the same wound (`ui/art.js WOUND_LOOKS`, `woundLook`; the sheet is `wound-presets.png`).

## 2026-11-18 · The core never reads the clock (purity pass)
- A new game's seed now comes from the game's boot and its New game button (`ui/seed.js`), not from inside the game logic. Nothing changes in play: a new game still gets a fresh seed from the clock, and saves are untouched (the seed was always stored in the save).
- `npm run screenshots` now plays the same game every run (seed 21, or `--seed <n>`): two runs gave the same 43 of 46 pictures byte for byte; the other three differ only by an animation caught at a slightly different moment.
- A new test (`tests/purity.test.js`) keeps the clock, `Math.random`, storage and the page out of the game logic and content, storage out of everything but the save code, and checks that the screens change the game only through `setState`.

## 2026-11-18 · The Jobs board prices fight jobs faster (speed pass)
- No rule, number, roll or wording changed: every read gives exactly the same answer, only sooner. Hack jobs got about twice as slow to price when the way out arrived (#194); this pass wins the time back.
- Measured, not changed: what a tap costs at a phone's speed (`drafts/NOTE-render-cost-2026-11-18.md`).
- The old fight engine's odds (the jobs still on it, such as the penthouse) use the same quick copy. Cold Jobs board reads on the same eight 30-week campaigns: median 73 to 40 ms, 90th percentile 453 to 340 ms (the code before #194 took 69 and 442 on the same boards).
- The planning fights (the odds the board and the plan screen show, and the van read) copy the fight with a plain copy instead of `structuredClone`, keeping shared the parts no round writes into (the orders, each runner's stats and build), and skip writing lines nobody reads. A round picks its target or patient by a single pass instead of sorting a copy of the list. Pricing the hack jobs over every crew, six difficulties and three risk levels: 5.6 s before, 3.9 s after (3.5 s before the way out); per team, data_pull 13.5 to 9.0 ms and quiet_hours 12.7 to 9.5 ms.

## 2026-11-17 · The visual pass: the Contagion jump, the tick, the shield, jam, the alarm and the medic's hands
UI only; no rule, number or roll changed. The stage draws more of what the rules already did.
- **The Contagion jumps.** When an infected enemy falls and the infection passes on, a green arc draws itself from the body to the new host, spores lift off the body, an orb leaps along the arc and the new host's ring flares as it lands. A chain is one hop a round.
- **The infection tick.** A green drip runs off the host, its ring flares and the pip it loses flashes green; a host that falls to the tick pops in a green ring, drops and leaves its body.
- **The shield.** A soft blue-white dome stands over who is covered, with an arc to them from the protector, held over the round and flaring when a shot lands on the protector in their place.
- **Jam.** A jam that takes sends a broken signal arc from the tech to each caller, with static and "Jammed" where it lands; a jam that fails is a flicker at the tech and "No lock".
- **The way out's alarm.** When a hack's work is done the camera takes in the door, red rings pulse out of it, the map's edge flashes red and the caption says "The alarm is up".
- **The medic's hands on the way out.** The aura's glow, then a soft green pulse sweeps out and lands a ring and a + on each runner reached (the patch); each runner steadied on the way out already got the aura picture.
- Lines now carry what these need (`jumpFrom`, `callers`, `targets`, `shieldedBy`, a tick's `max`), through `runfight.js feedLine` and `feed.js`. Tests: a shot- or overwatch-felled infected enemy hands the infection on; the planning read names the Contagion Vector and still says a drone can't be hurt by it; one test per effect.
- Preview: `node scripts/preview-livebattle.mjs <dir> --job data_pull --seed 3 --effect spread|tick|shield|jam|wayout` forges that effect's lines onto the round (`ui/fightstage.js forceEffect`). Frame rate 60 a second, also with `--throttle 4`. Captures in `docs/screenshots/2026-11-17-visual-pass/`.

## 2026-11-17 · The medic matters on the way out, and the aura is drawn
- **Medic on the way out.** When a hack's way out ends, a healer still standing gets one stabilise roll for each runner who is down (easier than in the fight, `FIGHT.wayOutTreatStep` -4) and gives every hurt runner who is up a pip (`wayOutTreatHeal`). A runner the medic reaches comes home without the serious injury. A team with a medic standing loses about 37% fewer recovery days on a hack than one without (data_pull 15.1 days against 24.1; `docs/sims/wayout-2026-11-17-medic-final.md`). A runner the medic has steadied no longer triggers the death's-door prompt on the way out. Wording is Claude's draft (`stabiliseWayOut`, `wayOutPatch`).
- **The medic's aura is now visible.** The aura line was never drawn (the stage did not receive the patient). Now a green glow grows round the medic and flashes twice, an orb crosses to the patient, a ring bursts where it lands, a "+" floats up and the pip fills with a flash. The medic healing themself bursts and fills the pip with nothing crossing. A stabilise or patch that lands is drawn the same way; a miss is still the red ring. Reduced motion cuts to the pip. Captures: `docs/screenshots/2026-11-17-medic-aura`.

## 2026-11-17 · Hack jobs end with a fight out (save v76)
- Finishing a hack no longer ends the fight. The alarm goes up and the opening force's kinds come in again, and the crew has to fight its way out: the fight is won when every enemy is down or running. The technician stops being the one to cover and fights with everyone else. Breaking off on the way out (someone down on cautious or steady, or pulling back at death's door) keeps the job and its pay, at the cost of whoever is down. Hack jobs are therefore longer and harder, and builds (Ricochet, the vectors) get a crowd to work on. Plan screen: "Then fight your way out." Wording is Claude's draft (`wayOut`, `end.wayOutWon`, `end.wayOutPulled`). Saves: a hack fight in progress still ends with the hack; v76. Sim: `docs/sims/vectors-2026-11-17-way-out.md`, `gate-teams-2026-11-17-way-out.md`.

## 2026-11-17 · Contagion, a second payload that jumps (draft, for the owner to read)
- A new build piece, the **Contagion Vector**, is a variation of the Cytotoxic Vector (both are vectors) and shares its payload slot, so a runner takes one or the other. A hit that gets past the armour infects, as before, but the infection lasts two rounds (the Vector lasts until the target falls). When an infected enemy goes down, the infection jumps to one other enemy of its group that is up, not already infected and not a machine. The jump is not a hit, so a plate does not stop it. Joss offers it once a runner has a medical of 8 or more. Numbers, wording and the offer text are drafts. No save change.

## 2026-11-17 · Missed shots line up the next one (new-engine fights)
- In a new-engine fight, each shot a runner misses in a row adds +2 to their next shot's roll (up to +6); a hit resets it. A bad streak now fixes itself instead of leaving a weak shooter with nothing to do. Numbers are `FIGHT.missBonus` and `missBonusCap` in `data/fights.js`. Old-engine fights (kidnap and the rest) are unchanged for now.
