# Changelog, 2026-11

Part of the changelog indexed in `CHANGELOG.md`. Newest first; add new entries at the top of the newest file. For *why* a decision was made, see the decision log in `DESIGN.md`.

## 2026-11-24 · Twists that fit the city
- **Routine work has no twists.** Linen runs, queue duty and the like no longer turn into police cordons or ambushes, and never show "intel unreliable". Careful crews end the first year with noticeably more money (about half as much again).
- **"Police tip-off" is now "Tip-off"**, and says who is waiting: Veridian-Apex security in Glass Heights, Racing Association security in the Basin, the Harbour Police elsewhere.
- **"Unexpected score" only turns up on break-ins** (Office Data Pull, Warehouse Lift, Casino Con, Penthouse Heist, Counterfeit Chrome, Data Scrub, Borrowed Glory, House Advantage), not on favours for people like Doc Siu or Mrs. Ip.
- **Letting a rival crew have the job no longer costs the client's trust**, like walking away from any other twist.

## 2026-11-23 · The double-cross twist is now an ambush
- **"Double-cross" is now "Ambush at the drop".** Clients no longer turn on the crew at the handover. Instead, the gang the job hurts is waiting on the way ("Silver Fan collectors are waiting on the way to the handover…"), or a few street toughs on a job against no gang. Same choices and odds: talk them down, hit first, or drop the goods and walk away (no pay, and the client doesn't blame the crew).

## 2026-11-23 · Faded traits join the crew updates
- **"No longer …" notes now join the day's Crew updates message** instead of each arriving as its own message, so a busy day with several traits fading is one entry in the inbox, not five. A trait tied to a client or district names it ("Pylon: no longer Client's Favourite (Mrs. Ip)").
- **The note says why.** A trait pushed out by a newer one says which: "Fog carries two passing traits at most, and Riding High has pushed out Client's Favourite, the older one." (It used to say "Something newer has taken its place.") Any other fade gives its reason: "Siphon is back to their usual self on that front: faced the police down."

## 2026-11-23 · New jobs: Salt in the Seals, Signed for the Shift, The Demonstration Passed, The Last Call-Out
- **Two new multi-map jobs from two new clients.** Lau Ching-yee (Morrow & Venn's marine claims surveyor) and Kwan Yiu-kit (AHL subcontractor accounts) now call with work. *Salt in the Seals* (Lau, the Docks, threat 3): trace seawater damage through a cargo lighter and a warehouse bay, then inspect the cartons at dawn before they're disposed of. *Signed for the Shift* (Kwan, the Docks, threat 3): a wage sheet marked paid, the workers' canteen and Lui Shuk-ping's yard office, then the yard before the morning shift. Both are fixed routes of two stops.
- **Two new jobs for Forewoman Tse** in Glass Heights (threat 2): *The Demonstration Passed* (test a drainage controller under real conditions before the cooperative signs for it) and *The Last Call-Out* (settle who covers the tower at night, and who gets paid).
- With three multi-map jobs, each now comes up about once every 4 to 5 weeks once unlocked (the weighting stays at 8; Lau and Kwan offer only their one job, so it is theirs whenever they call).
- The year-of-play save budget in the tests is 290k (was 270k): multi-map reports are longer. The save stays bounded by the last 20 full reports, not by how long a game runs.

## 2026-11-23 · "Trail" jobs are now multi-map jobs
- **Renamed under the hood: a job that moves across several maps is a multi-map job.** In a job file the block is `maps` (was `trail`), a stop's crisis is marked `stop: true` (was `trail: true`) and the last map is `finale` (was `held`). The engine is `core/multimap.js` (was `core/trail.js`). Nothing changes in play: Kidnap Rescue plays and reads exactly as before.
- **Lead-following is optional.** One `next` a stop makes a fixed route (a robbery in three places, a run of drops); several branch; shortcuts (`skip`) and wrong leads (`wrong`) are only for jobs about tracking someone down. Each job can set the watch's heading between maps (`maps.heading`): Kidnap Rescue keeps "On the trail", others say "On the move" unless they set their own.
- **New jobs can be multi-map.** `docs/new-job-prompt.md` has a "MULTI-MAP JOBS" section and a `Maps:` line in the pitch; `/add-job` knows them. A job file still written the old way is told it was renamed.
- Save version 89: a run part-way along its stops carries over.

## 2026-11-23 · Kidnap Rescue comes up more often

When Auntie Fung or Madam Rao calls with work, a job that follows a trail is eight times as likely to be the one they offer (`TRAIL_WEIGHT` in `core/jobs.js`). Any later trail job gets the same weighting.

## 2026-11-22 · Kidnap Rescue follows a trail

Each stop is a crisis on its own map, with an abort as always. A strong result can take them straight to where he's held; a failed one can send them to a wrong lead (a fish wholesaler's van, a frightened woman with his handset) before the trail picks up again.
- **That night, the rescue** plays as before: the closed cha chaan teng, the lookout, the wrong floor, the fight to the van.
- The watch screen changes map at each stop, with the time and the place as a heading ("15:40 · a rented lock-up behind the weighbridge"); the report reads as the trail, then the night.
- Sent hands-off, the crew follows the trail on the first day and goes in on the last.
- The trail's text is Claude's draft for the owner to mark. Save version 88.

## 2026-11-22 · Expeditions
- **Send a crew after something the tong wants:** Jobs › **Trips** has three goals: **a weapon** (6 days), **cash** (4 days) or **a recruit** (5 days). Pick the district (the Docks or the Night Market for now) and up to three runners; the plan bar says how likely they are to get it.
- **A line a day:** day 1 names a contact or a place, the days between find leads, hit dead ends and snags, turn up something on the side (cash, a piece for the stash, a rumour that helps jobs in that district for a week), and the last day turns and closes. The latest line shows on the trip's card (Jobs › Trips and City's Out now) with **The log** to open.
- **What they bring home:** a weapon hunt brings the named Custom or Premium piece it was after (or a lesser one); a cash run the sum (or half); a recruit hunt a pro or veteran willing to meet you in Crew › Leads (or a rookie's name). A failed trip brings only what it found.
- **When a deal goes wrong** they call you: watch the fight, let them fight it on steady orders, or pull out (everyone home). You can pull a trip out on any other day too.
- No pay and no fame; fights on a trip count in the fight record. At most two trips out at once. Save version 87.
- The Jobs sub-tabs fit on one row at phone width.

## 2026-11-22 · The adventure log, slice 1
- **Crews out send back a line a day.** A crew on a hands-off job writes one every day out: casing the place, setting up, waiting for the right night, or a small snag. The line names who did it (whoever is best at what the day needed), and in the Docks and the Night Market the people and places there. About one day in three adds a line in the runner's own character (a superstitious runner leaves an orange at a shrine); now and then a day is about two of the crew together, or Joss chips in.
- **Where it shows:** the crew-out card on City leads with the day's line, with the job's own beat under it; **The log** under the card opens every day so far. The report lists **The days out** above its timeline.
- **Side tasks** of two or three days write a line on each day out; the report ends on the task's result. The side-work cards on City show the latest line and the log.
- **Posts** keep their own lines and rolls and now and then add a line in the runner's character.
- Every log keeps its last 10 lines. Save version 86 (older saves upgrade on load).
- The new day lines (`src/data/advlog/jobDays.js`, `sideDays.js`) are Claude's drafts for the owner to mark. The Docks and Night Market files moved from `drafts/advlog/` into the game.

## 2026-11-21 · Post fights count in the fight record
- Everyone who stands in a post's fight, the crew on the post and any help that gets there, now has it counted in their record: fights, fights won, a crewmate kept alive, a serious wound survived. It never counts as a job, so fame, standing and active weeks don't move.

## 2026-11-21 · Ambushes on posts are fights
- When a post is hit, the call now offers three answers. **Watch the fight** plays it on the district's map with the fight screen you know (orders and risk each round). **Let them fight it** has the crew fight on steady orders and sends the result back. **Give it up** brings everyone home unhurt, as before.
- **Send help:** the call lists anyone free, with the round they'd arrive (2 from a hall in the same district, 3 across town, 4 to or from the Heights). Pick up to two. They join the fight on that round, go home after it, and can't take a job that day.
- Three attackers in Old Town, more in tougher districts. Win and the post carries on with a bonus and +2 trust; anyone badly hurt comes off it and the rest stay on. Pull back and the post ends, with the pay so far kept.
- Same safety as job fights: on cautious and steady nobody dies without being asked. Post fights earn no fame or standing, like posts.
- Save version 85: a call saved before this gets the new answers.

## 2026-11-21 · Security posts
- New on Jobs › Side: **Posts**. Each Monday three clients you know offer a post: Guard (a door or a loading bay, 1-2 runners, a week), Escort (a courier or cargo across a district, up to 3 runners, 2-4 days) and Watch (a stall row or a mahjong hall, 1-2 runners, a week). Pay is per runner per week by district (¥450 in Old Town up to ¥700 in the Heights; an escort pays half as much again for its days), builds day by day and is paid on Sunday with the bills.
- Pick who goes on the post's own screen: slots across the top, free runners below with the two stats that matter, and the chance each day goes quietly.
- While they're out, the post shows its last log line ("Deliveries came and went. Drizzle checked every one."). Some days go well (a tip), some go rough (a light injury or heat). If the post is hit, they call you: hold it, with the odds shown (if it fails someone is badly hurt and the week's pay is lost), or give it up and everyone comes home. Nothing on a post can kill anyone.
- Posts teach a little but earn no fame or standing. You can pull runners off early (trust -3, the pay so far kept). Finishing a post builds the client's trust.
- Save version 84: older saves get an empty list and their first posts on Monday. The post text is a first draft for the owner to mark.
