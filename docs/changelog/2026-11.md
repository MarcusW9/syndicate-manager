## 2026-11-14 · Tier colour on the picture's outline
- **An item's picture is now outlined in its tier's colour** (green Verified, blue Custom, purple Premium) in the Armoury, the shop, the worn tiles and the bag. Standard keeps the plain frame.
- **The tier tag is gone wherever the picture shows the tier.** It stays where there is no picture: the job report's gear line and a runner's carried list.
- **A worn tile's perk names take the tier's colour** (they were always blue).
- **Fix:** tapping a perk on a job report that had just played in did nothing the first time; it now opens.

## 2026-11-14 · Perks on Custom and Premium gear
- **Blue Custom and purple Premium gear now drop from jobs.** About 1 completed main job in 3 still drops something; of the drops, Verified is 28%, Custom 10%, Premium 5% (4 and the 1 that would be Apex, which is not in the game yet) and the rest Standard. They are never in the shop, the weekly shelf or on recruits.
- **They carry perks.** A Custom item has one, a Premium item two different ones. Both give the same +1 as Verified; the perk is the extra. Perks are small and sideways, never more of what the item already does: a +1 in another stat (cool, stealth, tech, medical, piloting), a little less chance of a serious wound, +1 towards patching one, +1 armour or +1 damage in a fight, hits that get through some armour, one more overwatch shot or drone action, a rifle that draws no heat, an implant that lets neural load rest 5 lower.
- **Perks show up where the item does:** under its row in the Armoury stash, the gear sheet and a runner's carried list, as names on the worn tiles, and on the job report's gear line. Tap a perk's name for its line. Rolls that use a perk's bonus name it, like a trait.
- **Stacking is held down:** across everything a runner carries, perks give at most +2 in one stat, 10 points of serious-wound guard, +2 patch, +1 armour, +1 pierce and +1 damage.
- **The fence pays more:** Custom x3.5 and Premium x5 the Standard price (Verified is x2.5). A perk does not change the price.
- **Phone fix:** on a screen 480px wide or less, a gear row's button now sits on its own row under the picture and text, so the item's name and tier tag are no longer squeezed into a narrow column (the Verified tag was clipped at 360px).
- **Old saves load as they are** (save version 65).

## 2026-11-13 · Gear rarity tiers and drops on successful jobs
- **Gear now has five rarity tiers, with colours:** Standard (grey), Verified (green), Custom (blue), Premium (purple) and Apex (gold). Standard is what Street was and Verified is what Pro was, same prices and same +1. Custom, Premium and Apex are set up but do not exist in play yet.
- **Every item shows its tier** as a small coloured tag with the tier's name in it, in the Armoury (stash, shop and this week's guns), on a runner's loadout and the gear sheet, and on the job report.
- **A job you finish clean may leave gear.** About 1 in 3 completed main jobs (35%) drops an item into the stash: the rarity is rolled first, then the item. Nothing drops on an abort or a job that lost someone, and side tasks never drop. Hands-off jobs do.
- **No drop means scrap.** The crew fences it for ¥50. The report says which one it was, in one line.
- **Old saves upgrade:** gear called Street or Pro is renamed Standard or Verified (save version 64).

## 2026-11-11 · What moves the left-behind rate (sensitivity sim, no game change)
- `scripts/sim-vanlevers.mjs` and `docs/sims/vanlevers-2026-11-11.md`: the two holdExtract jobs at their real difficulty, the game's own starting rosters (the planning read's best team), the defaults hands-off, 400 seeds, one van number changed at a time in the script only. Declared bar: cut "someone left behind" by 10+ points without raising downs by more than 0.2 or heat by more than 0.3.
- **Base (steady): someone is left behind in 56% of extractions** (fresh roster 39-46%, authored 62-78%), 1.7 downs a fight, clean wins 10%.
- **The van arriving later is the lever that works:** round 4 instead of 3 cuts it to 37% (-19 points, downs -0.45, heat -0.14); round 5 to 27% (-29, downs -0.63). Clean wins drop a little (10% to 8% and 7%).
- **Door slots do nothing** (4 or 5 slots: no change; the door is never the bottleneck). **The van waiting longer barely helps** (waits 5, 6 or 8: -2 to -4 points, and about +0.1 downs). **Holding the van three rounds: no change.**
- **Easier boarding makes it worse** (boarding step -2: +4 points left behind; 0: +8). Not understood yet; runners probably leave cover for the van sooner and are shot in the open.
- **Committed risk on starting crews kills far more than the target:** someone dies in 15-49% of these extractions (fresh Warehouse 49%, Protection 37%; authored 22% and 15%), against about 1 in 25 for the stronger sim crews in the gate.

## 2026-11-11 · Hits land, and the stamp has weight

## 2026-11-11 · Skirmishes play out (no three-round stand-off)
- **A fight that is not a raid no longer stops after three rounds.** It used to call the crew in with "Push past them (alert +1, heat +1) or Pull back" while guards were still up, even for a lone runner who was winning. Now it plays until the guards are down, a runner is at death's door (the usual call-in) or the crew's orders pull back. A 12-round backstop still calls in a true stalemate. Raids keep their three rounds ("Hold the door"), and a fight already in progress in an old save keeps the cap it had.
- **Side effect, for the owner:** with the cap gone, the engine-1 test fight is won either way, so what commanding a fight buys is health, not wins (about 14% less health lost, 2,199 against 2,569 over 400 fights; before, orders won 309 of 400 against 246). The planning read's stand-off share is about zero for these fights.

## 2026-11-11 · The validation gate on the real code (battle rework, slice 3): verdict neither
