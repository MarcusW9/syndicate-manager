# Changelog, 2026-11

Part of the changelog indexed in `CHANGELOG.md`. Newest first; add new entries at the top of the newest file. For *why* a decision was made, see the decision log in `DESIGN.md`.

## 2026-11-17 · Sim: Cytotoxic against Contagion
- New `scripts/sim-vectors.mjs` (report `docs/sims/vectors-2026-11-17.md`) plays the same teams with each vector, alone and with Ricochet, on the job's own fight, a six-hired-gun swarm and a mixed fight. The two vectors win within a point of each other; Contagion kills more by the tick and does jump. Neither moves results much (Ricochet does), and neither does anything to a swarm of 1-pip enemies. No game change.

## 2026-11-17 · Contagion, a second payload that jumps (draft, for the owner to read)
- A new build piece, the **Contagion Vector**, is a variation of the Cytotoxic Vector (both are vectors) and shares its payload slot, so a runner takes one or the other. A hit that gets past the armour infects, as before, but the infection lasts two rounds (the Vector lasts until the target falls). When an infected enemy goes down, the infection jumps to one other enemy of its group that is up, not already infected and not a machine. The jump is not a hit, so a plate does not stop it. Joss offers it once a runner has a medical of 8 or more. Numbers, wording and the offer text are drafts. No save change.

## 2026-11-17 · The planning read gives Joss's advice on builds
- On the plan screen of a new-engine fight, when someone on the squad carries a build, Joss now gives one line of advice, closed to start. For example: "Drizzle's Cytotoxic Vector can't hurt the drones, because they are machines. A tech who can jam them would help." Tap it to open the breakdown: what the fight throws (a group, machines, armour) and what each build can do about it. It reads the same sample fight and tags the rules use, so it cannot disagree with the fight. Nothing shows when nobody has a build. Wording is Claude's draft (`build.*` keys in `data/fightText.js`), the owner's to rewrite. No save change.

## 2026-11-17 · An offer is flagged where you look for it
- A runner with an offer waiting (a build piece or a specialisation) now shows an **Offer** tag on their Crew roster row, and the **Crew** tab carries a count of runners with an offer waiting, so it no longer hides in the inbox. On the runner's page the Offers panel moved to the top, above their bio. Taking it or "Not yet" clears all three.

## 2026-11-17 · Fourteen more jobs fight on the new engine
- Kidnap Rescue, Casino Con, Clinic Night, Mahjong Ledger, Container 409, Counterfeit Chrome, Low Water, Mooring Watch, Count Against Paper, Quarter to Two, Manual Handling, The Favourite, Last Ticket and Wrong Horse now play their fights on the new engine (orders, pips, the live map, builds and the miss bonus). Hold the hack: casino, chrome, count against paper, wrong horse. Hold and extract: kidnap, ledger, container 409, low water, quarter to two, manual handling, last ticket. Hold the yard: clinic, mooring watch, favourite.
- No waves written for them yet; each fights the district's own force. Sim: `docs/sims/gate-teams-2026-11-17-fourteen.md` (small run: 6 campaigns); every safety bar holds, Mooring Watch is the hardest for an unbuilt crew (26% wins).

## 2026-11-17 · Missed shots line up the next one (new-engine fights)
- In a new-engine fight, each shot a runner misses in a row adds +2 to their next shot's roll (up to +6); a hit resets it. A bad streak now fixes itself instead of leaving a weak shooter with nothing to do. Numbers are `FIGHT.missBonus` and `missBonusCap` in `data/fights.js`. Old-engine fights (kidnap and the rest) are unchanged for now.
