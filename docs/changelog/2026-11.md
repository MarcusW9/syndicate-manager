# Changelog, 2026-11

Part of the changelog indexed in `CHANGELOG.md`. Newest first; add new entries at the top of the newest file. For *why* a decision was made, see the decision log in `DESIGN.md`.

## 2026-11-21 · Triage tags for the way-out patch
- When a hack's way out ends and the healer treats the crew, glowing data links run from the medic to each runner reached, with packets flowing down them, and a cut-corner tag lands on each runner: the amount healed and STABLE (green) or STILL HURT (amber), with a quick glitch as it appears. The healer's stabilise roll on a downed runner gets its own tag, STABILISED or NOT STABLE. Tags stagger in height so neighbours do not hide each other. Picture only: no rules or save changes.

## 2026-11-21 · The van's clock ring
- When the getaway van pulls in, a segmented HUD ring opens round it: one segment for each round until it leaves, cyan at first and flashing red on the last, with targeting brackets, a slowly turning scan ring and a glitching label (VAN T-4, T-3 ...). A square pip lights for each runner who gets aboard. If the van is held, the ring grows two segments and says HELD +2; if it goes with someone outside, the label says LEFT BEHIND; when it goes, the ring flickers out.
- Ability Lab: the patch pulse added to compare (radar sweep, nano-mist and lock-on, triage tags); not built in the game yet. Picture only: no rules or save changes.

## 2026-11-20 · Level-ups
- Runners level up instead of waiting for Joss's offers. Once a runner has 4 jobs with the crew and a skill is ready for them, they level up: a notice arrives, their row on Crew says Level up, and the Crew tab counts them. On their page the Skills list shows a Level up banner; open any Ready skill and tap Learn. The pick waits until you make it. The next level-up comes 4 jobs after each pick, and a runner can hold one move, one payload and one specialisation, so at most three in a career.
- A specialisation's jobs on overwatch now show on the list as a gate of their own (for example "Rifle jobs 1/3"), so you can see what is left.
- Joss's line about each skill now shows when you open a Ready one. "Not yet" is gone. Saves upgrade (v83): an offer still open in the mail is closed with a note, and the next level-up counts from the last skill taken.

## 2026-11-20 · Training focus
- Each runner's page has a Training focus under Development: tap up to two stats. From then on 15% of what they learn in other stats goes to those two instead. They still learn from the work they do, and nobody grows past what they have in them. Over a season that is about two extra points in a side stat with room to grow. Saves upgrade (v82; nobody starts with a focus).

## 2026-11-20 · The crew's shots miss less on hard jobs
- On harder jobs the crew's shots used to miss about half the time (enemies landed about 70% of theirs). Shooting now feels only two thirds of a job's extra difficulty, so shots on threat 4-5 jobs miss about 30% of the time; rookie jobs are unchanged and enemies shoot as before. A runner who misses also lines up the next shot more (+3 a miss in a row, up to +9), so long runs of misses are rare: a run of three now comes in about 1 hard fight in 30, down from 1 in 4 or 5. Measured in `docs/sims/misses-2026-11-20*.md`.
