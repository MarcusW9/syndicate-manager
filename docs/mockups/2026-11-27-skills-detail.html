Skills and traits in detail

What the reference does well: held, ready and locked are three clear looks; a lock says exactly what it needs ("13 / 15"); small nodes hang off big ones; and two numbers sit at the top (level and reputation). Here those ideas are applied to Jax with the skills, requirements and stats in the game today, in our own skin.
