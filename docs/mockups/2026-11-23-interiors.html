Generated interiors · mockup

Each map is built from a seed (the job and the stop), so a stop always looks the same and nothing goes in the save. Rooms, doors, furniture and the route are generated; the drawing is the game's own. Furniture is the fight cover and is never placed on the way through. Three seeds of each kind.

Lock-up · the Docks

One big shed with a roller door, the van parked inside, shelving and crates, one or two rooms partitioned off. The target is the room furthest in.

Mahjong parlour · Night Market

Tables at the front, then a kitchen, a store, stairs, and the back room as far from the street as the rooms go. A neon sign over the door.

Tenement stairwell · Old Town

A stair core up from the street door, flats off both sides, the target one of the flats at the top.

Alley · Old Town

A winding way between blocks from the street to a back door, narrowed by bins, AC units and crates.
