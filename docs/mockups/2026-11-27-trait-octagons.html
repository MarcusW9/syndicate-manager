The same plan as the ring mockup, with no round shapes. The octagon is the badge you picked; the rank is a segmented gauge, as every gauge in the game is. Jax a year in: six badges on the page, the rest behind "All 9".

O3 · States, on the recommended look

Rank 1 to 3 fills the gauge left to right. "Lately" uses the same gauge as time left, draining right to left. Born has none. When rank 3 turns into the runner's nature it becomes gold, like the specialisations, and loses the gauge: it isn't going anywhere.

Yellow corner: new or deepened this week. Rank arrives as news; nothing shows how close the next one is.
