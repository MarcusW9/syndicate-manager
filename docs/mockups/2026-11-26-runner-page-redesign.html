A runner, from first principles

What a player wants to know about one of their people, in order: who they are now, are they getting better, what they can do, what happened to them. The recommendation follows the pushback: one progress number (the level), stats in words-and-bars, the person before the numbers, no power. Then four other ways to lay out the same runner, each drawn from scratch. Same runner throughout: Lychee, 22, infiltrator, level 5.

Recommended

R1 · The runner's page: the person first

Top to bottom in the order a player asks. A sentence from Joss says who they are now. One level, one bar. "What they've become" (skills, traits, partner, setbacks) comes before the stats. Stats are a quiet grid with next-point bars. The road at the bottom ties every level to where it was earned.

Football Manager's scout report taken all the way: the runner is a page of prose built from their history and traits, with the level in the margin. Numbers fold away. Most atmosphere, least at-a-glance; growth has to be written well to land.

  • What moves between them: where the level sits (beside the name, a ring, a corner, a path); whether stats lead (V3, V4) or follow the person (R1, V1); whether history is a list (R1), the main view (V2) or prose (V1); whether power exists at all (only V3).
  • The recommendation mixes: R1's order (person, level, what they've become, stats, road), V2's road at the bottom, R2's report cards. V1's prose could replace Joss's one line later; V4's card could be the roster on a tablet.
  • Assumed in R: one level (no power); level 1–10 picks are a skill or a stat push (more growth in a stat you choose, under the ceiling), not a flat +1; the tier word follows the level. Each is the owner's call.
  • "Light-Fingered", "Synergy", "Overconfident" and "Spotter's Eye" are real traits and skills; the trip that earns a trait (Light-Fingered from the weapon hunt) is not in the game yet.
  • Tap targets: rows, chips that open, buttons and stat cells are 44px or taller on a phone. Nothing is on hover only.
