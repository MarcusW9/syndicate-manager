The days out, round two

More takes on 1 (spaced) and 2 (rail). No colour for how a day went. Quiet days are a short filler line; the flavour goes on days where something happens. A bar fills as they work. Days you can go in and watch (an encounter on a map, a fight) and call-ins are marked, which is where multi-map comes in.

The trip: a weapon hunt in the Night Market, 6 days, needs 3 leads. Day 4 a deal went wrong and they called you; day 6 is the meet, which you can watch.

  • How this maps to the game. The leads bar is an expedition's progress against its goal's need (data/expeditions.js). Quiet days are the progress rolls that miss ("Nothing moved", tone plain); they'd get their own short filler pool in core/advlog.js.
  • Days you can watch are what long jobs already do: one stop a day, and "watch by day" calls you at a stop (core/autorun.js watchByDay, core/prep.js, multi-map in core/multimap.js). For expeditions it would be new: a planned day with a map (the meet) shown ahead of time, plus the existing call when a deal goes wrong (startExpeditionFight).
  • "See the fight again" needs the fight's record kept with the trip; today a watched fight is in the fight record, not linked from the log.
  • The day text on days 2 and 4 is made up for the mockup.
