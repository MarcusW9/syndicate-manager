Runner levels: five looks

Stats stay 1 to 20 and still drive every roll. On top, each runner has a level from 1 to 12, filled by every job, trip and side task, so nobody comes back without moving. Each level is a pick (the existing level-up). Each stat also shows how close its next point is. Same people throughout: Vector (rookie muscle) and Lychee (rookie infiltrator), home from a six-day weapon hunt.

  • Stats don't change. 1–20, a d20 plus a stat on every roll; the risk reads, threat bands and fights stay as they are. The level is on top: it gives picks, not dice.
  • One experience pool. The level is filled by the same raw growth gainXp already hands out, from jobs, trips and side work alike. Today level-ups count main jobs only (every 4, data/builds.js LEVEL_UP); this replaces that count.
  • Next-point bars are free. Each runner already keeps how far each stat is toward its next point (op.progress); B and A just draw it.
  • Open numbers (owner): how many levels (12 shown); how much a trip is worth next to a main job (shown here as roughly a third of a level for six days); whether rookie / pro / veteran stay tied to the main stat (as now) or move to levels.
  • Numbers here are illustrative: at today's growth rate a six-day trip moves a runner about 4 raw points, far less than shown. The tuning comes first (the review's build 1).
  • Tap targets: the whole crew card, the stat boxes, the picks and the button are 44px or taller on a phone (the small badge on the card is not a target of its own). Nothing is on hover only.
