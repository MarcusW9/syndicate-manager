Ability containers: ten looks

The same five things in every look, one of each kind: a skill picked (Ricochet), an earned trait (Corner Fighter), a partner (with Static), something lately (Short Fuse, wearing off) and a born trait (Loyal). Colour by kind throughout: cyan skill, green earned, violet partner, orange lately, grey born.
