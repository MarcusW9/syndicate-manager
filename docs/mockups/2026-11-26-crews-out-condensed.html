  • A calling crew is never folded away in any of the three: it is open (A), first (B), or red (C), and the call is also in Today's Needs you.
  • Buttons: filled buttons repeated per crew compete with each other and with Continue. In the revision every per-crew action is a light link with the colour on the row edge; the full call screen (one at a time) keeps its big Take the call, as Today's Needs you does.
  • My pick: A, revised. It shows every crew's faces at once, reads top to bottom like the rest of the game, and opens in place with no new pattern. C is tighter but adds a sheet the game doesn't use elsewhere; B hides all but one crew.
  • Rows and tiles are 44px or taller to tap. Faces are placeholders for the game's portraits.
