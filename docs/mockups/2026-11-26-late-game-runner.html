No "recommended power" on jobs: the threat level and Joss's read already say it, from the stats the job actually needs.

  • Power = earned + gear. Earned is the lifetime total of the raw growth gainXp hands out (jobs, trips, side work), times ten; it never falls and costs one field on each runner (a save bump). Gear is worked out from what they carry, by tier (data/gear.js TIERS), never saved. Neither part is used for any roll, pick, pay or forecast.
  • The level comes from earned power only (gear can't give a level): each level costs a bit more power than the last, so a runner who lasts years reaches 30 to 40. One number underneath, two ways to show it.
  • Levels 11+ give veteran's picks: small and sideways (a district or kind-of-work edge with a cap, teaching, keeping a trait, slow growth past a wall). They need writing; start with a short repeatable list.
  • The hidden rating stays hidden (core/rating.js: "never shown"). Power isn't the rating: two runners with the same power can be very different today, and the scout's words still say how good someone is.
  • Names (power, titles, the picks) are placeholders; final words go through WRITING_GUIDE.md.
  • Tap targets: the picks, the button, the roster rows and the power line are 44px or taller on a phone. Nothing is on hover only.
