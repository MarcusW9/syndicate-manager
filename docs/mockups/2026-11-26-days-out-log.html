The days out: six looks

The log on a trip report, which today reads as one block of text. Same seven entries in each look. The game already knows each day's tone (plain, good, warn), the stat rolled and the turn day; only looks 2 to 5 use them. Phone width first.

0 · Today, for comparison

One rule down the left, the day label inline in small grey caps, 6px between days. The labels disappear into the text.

The days out

  1. Day 1 Over a shared table, Vector mentioned we were looking for a rifle called the Scaled Typhoon. Kwong Wai-shun wrote a seller's name on the receipt.

  3. Day 3 A repairer was laid up after an implant flare-up. Vector kept the visit short and came away with a name.
