Fight readability + ricochet

Mockups for the two playtest notes. Everything here is plain SVG on the game's own skin. Nothing is wired into the game yet. Tap Play on each card.

A · Who just did what

Same five shots in the same order on every card. Today a shot lasts about a quarter of a second and nothing says who fired.

Now

0 · Current pace

Tracers only. The eye has to guess the shooter.
Proposal 1

1 · Spotlight beat

 
Each action gets a beat. The rest of the map dims, and the actor gets a ring and a name tag with the move. The shot flies. The result floats up and holds (HIT −12 or MISS). About 1.5s per action. Tap-to-step suits this one.
Proposal 2

2 · Order strip + light spotlight

 
A strip above the map lists the round's actions in order. The current chip lifts and finished ones keep a red (hit) or grey (miss) underline. A plain sentence under the map says what happened. The map only gets a ring on the actor, so it stays quicker (about 1s per action). On the real screen the strip would sit in the commentary slot the stage already feeds (watch.js commentary).

B · Ricochet

Today: a white dot on a straight line, a spark on landing. Ten crates deep in a round, you can't tell where it has been. Same three targets on every card.

Now

0 · Current ricochet

Proposal 1

1 · Comet trail + numbered impacts

A glowing head with a long fading trail. Each landing gets a hit-stop (the target freezes for a beat), a shock ring, sparks, a numbered badge (1, 2, 3) and the damage float. After the last hit the whole route glows for a second so you can read the path.
Proposal 2

2 · Plot the route, then fire

Targets lock on one at a time with a reticle and a number, joined by a dashed route. Then the round runs it. Each leg turns solid as it's flown. You know what will happen before it does. This is the most legible and the longest (about 4.5s).
Proposal 3

3 · Cover kiss

The round actually ricochets: it clips the edge of a bit of cover on the way to a target, with a spark and a tink ring at the kink, so the angled path looks physical. Reads as "a ricochet" at a glance even with the sound off. Costs a kink point per leg (staging picks the nearest cover edge, picture only).

My pick

A: 1 for shots that matter (hits that crack plates, falls, specials), 2's strip for ordinary ones. Add the tap-to-step toggle to the Watch pace. B: 1 as the default, with 3's cover kink if you want it to feel more physical. 2 is great but long, so I'd keep it for a showcase beat only.
