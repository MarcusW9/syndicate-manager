# How often the crew's shots miss (2026-11-20-v2-twothirds)

**Rules played:** climb share 0.67, softener +2 a miss to +6.

`node scripts/sim-misses.mjs 8 20 2026-11-20-v2-twothirds`. Measurement only: nothing changes. Real fight code, hands-off, every job in `FIGHT_JOBS` (77), each with the best team by combat (a tech on a hack, a medic on a team of 3 or more) of a campaign roster grown to the job's gate (8 campaigns), 20 fights a roster a job a risk. Difficulty: `fightDiff(rollTarget(job))`, the crises' own. Campaigns reaching each threat's gate: threat 1 8/8, threat 2 8/8, threat 3 8/8, threat 4 8/8, threat 5 8/8.

- **Miss:** the roll failed. **Crack:** the roll passed but a plate took it all (no HP off). **Hit:** HP came off.
- **Roll chance:** the average chance the rolls had, the bad-luck softener included (+2 a miss in a row, to +6). Today's rule: d20 + combat (+ softener) against 10 + difficulty -2 + cover (half 2, full 4).
- **Rounds with nothing landed:** rounds in which the crew fired at least once and took no HP off anyone.
