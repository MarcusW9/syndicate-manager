## What it says (Claude's read, 2026-11-17)

- **The two vectors play almost the same.** On every job and shape the clean-win rate differs by at most a point. The Contagion Vector kills 1.5 to 2 times as many enemies by the tick (0.15 against 0.04 on the warehouse, 0.28 against 0.23 on data_pull) and jumps 0.1 to 0.5 times a fight, so it is a little better where it does anything, never worse.
- **Neither vector moves the result much.** Alone they add 1 to 3 points of clean wins on the job's own fight. Ricochet is the build that matters: +38 points on a six-hired-gun swarm at protection and warehouse, +11 on the job's own fight.
- **The swarm does not feed Contagion.** In the swarm shape both vectors kill 0.00 a fight. A hired gun has one pip, so any hit that lands drops them, and an infection only takes on a target the hit leaves up. A drone has a plate, so a first hit only cracks it. The spread needs targets with two or more pips (security guards, enforcers) or a boss. This is the finding to act on: the "contagion through a swarm" idea needs a swarm kind with 2 pips, or a vector that also takes on a cracked plate.
- **The vector wins more where enemies have 2 pips:** the mixed shape (two guards and two drones added) and the warehouse's enforcers show the highest vector kills (0.46 a fight with Ricochet).
- **The tuning levers do not bite yet.** A 2-round limit against "until it falls" changes nothing here because the enemies it meets have 1 or 2 pips; there is nothing to tune until a 3-pip enemy exists.
- **Limits:** 8 campaigns, 60 fights a roster, four jobs, steady only. "Swarm" and "mixed" are far harder than any real job (best team clean-win 10% and 2%), so read their differences between builds, not their levels. No death in any row.
