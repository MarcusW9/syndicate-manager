Share 0 is today's fight before the way out (it ends with the hack). "Job done" counts a clean win or a break-off on the way out (the objective is kept); "clean" is a win with nobody breaking off; "downs" is runners down a fight (each is a serious injury, 21 days out).

## What it says (Claude's read, 2026-11-17)

- **The job still gets done at the same rate.** Job done is 73% before and 72% with the way out on data_pull (79% and 78% on quiet_hours): breaking off on the way out keeps the objective, so the sim's old "clean win" is now rare (5% on data_pull) and no longer the measure.
- **The cost is injuries.** Runners down a fight go from 0.35 to about 1.0 on data_pull and from 0.26 to 0.7 on quiet_hours, so nearly every hack now leaves someone with a serious injury (21 days out). No death in any row.
- **The size of the new group barely matters.** One enemy (share 0.2, `wayout-2026-11-17-small.md`) gives 0.96 downs, two give 0.96, three give 1.01. The cost is that the crew must now beat every enemy, including those still up from the hack, after spending its pips protecting the technician.
- **Builds help.** Ricochet and the Contagion Vector together cut downs from 0.96 to 0.70 to 0.80 and lift clean wins from 5% to 21% to 30% (data_pull, share 0.2 to 0.34): the way out is where a build earns its place.
- **What this means for the default.** The agreed default was the same again (share 1). That, and 0.67, tipped a hands-off year of play (`tests/inbox.test.js`), so the default is half (0.5). The owner's call remains: accept about one injury a hack job, or soften it (lighter injuries on the way out, or no break-off at the first down).
- **Limits:** 8 campaigns, 60 fights a roster, two hack jobs, steady only. `vectors-2026-11-17-way-out.md` was run with the full group (share 1).
