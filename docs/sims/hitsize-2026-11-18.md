**Read this first:** no sim equips the gear a campaign finds, so the crews here carry what they start or are hired with (mostly bare hands, damage 2, which hits exactly as before; a few damage-3 sidearms now hit for 13). What changed in them is mostly the wobble on 20 HP hits (±3, was ±2), which also moves every later roll on the same seed. The rule's real effect, a crew with found guns, is not measured by any sim yet.

## The gate teams (best team, 26 jobs)

- **No death on steady or cautious anywhere** (0.0% on every row, before and after).
- **Job done:** within 3 points on every job (mean +0.2).
- **Left behind on steady:** down 1-3 points on the yard and van jobs (warehouse 8% → 6%, dead_air 9% → 6%, kidnap 8% → 6%, the_last_collection 11% → 9%).
- **Downs:** the same or a little lower on almost every row.
- **Deaths on committed (the accepted risk):** mostly within a point either way; up on two hack jobs, **data_pull 8.7% → 11.5%** and **quiet_hours 8.6% → 10.0%**, both already over the declared 8% before this change; down on the_last_collection 4.7% → 3.1%. still_connected (13.4% → 12.5%) and the_wrong_fare (11.2% → 11.3%) stay over 8%.

## The vectors and the way out

- Way out (hack jobs): wins up about 4 points (data_pull 73% → 77%, quiet_hours 79% → 83%), downs a fight a little lower, rounds up about 0.3.
- Vectors: the builds' order is unchanged (Ricochet + Contagion still best in a crowd); vector kills a fight up slightly, since more bodies survive a wobbled hit for a tick to finish.
