# Step 0: the battle rework's paper rules, played by script (2026-11-11, profile hard)

Generated by `node scripts/sim-step0.mjs 400 2026-11-11 hard`. Profile **hard** (four enemies at the start). The paper rules of `drafts/SPEC-battle-orders.md` with the first values of `drafts/NUMBERS-battle-orders.md`, standalone (not the game's fight code). 400 seeds per cell; every cell is in `step0-2026-11-11-hard.csv`.

Fights are hands-off (no dilemmas raised; their conditions are counted). Risk is steady unless stated.

**Owner calls used:** planned damage that covers an enemy stops later attackers; no talk; no overwatch; smoke = cover +1 step for everyone; the early van costs +1 heat; a runner withdrawing alone takes the shots aimed at them that round and dies if they are downed while leaving.

**Sim assumptions** (not in the spec; each needs a call before code):
- Threat 3 for every fight (base difficulty 14, three enemies at the start).
- A successful jam cancels every backup and alarm shown this round and takes out one drone (the lowest-numbered).
- An unanswered alarm in a pressure fight (holdExtract) brings the next pressure arrival one round earlier.
- Specials (smoke, the early van) cost no action. Stims are not carried. No overwatch.
- Crew pull-back (someone down on a hands-off fight, the tech leaving, the deadline) ends the fight with no parting shots.
- In holdExtract a runner going down ends the fight as a retreat even when the van is there (boarding is the objective).
- At the deadline the objective has failed, whatever the risk level (pressing on is not modelled).
- Sidearm aim bonuses are left out; muscle carries a revolver (strong hit on a margin of 8), gunners a heritage pistol (10), everyone else a street piece (12).

**Plans:** `attack` (everyone attacks; the tech works in holdHack; boards only when nothing is left to attack), `hold` (hunker when shot at; board in stage 1), `protect` (everyone shields the tech / the lowest-pips runner; one protector while boarding), `support` (patch, jam, shield others), `defaults` (the spec's crew-made orders), `mixed` (one protector, the rest attack radio sources first).
