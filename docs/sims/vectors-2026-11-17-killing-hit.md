## What it says (Claude's read, 2026-11-17, after the killing hit counts)

Same sim and seeds as `vectors-2026-11-17.md`; the only change is that a killing hit from a Contagion carrier counts as infecting the target for an instant, so it jumps at once.

- **The swarm now feeds Contagion.** In the six-hired-gun swarm it jumps 1.3 to 3.2 times a fight with Ricochet at protection and warehouse (0.00 before) and kills 0.6 to 0.8 a fight by the tick. Clean wins there: 83% against 77% with the Cytotoxic Vector at protection, 74% against 74% at warehouse (but someone left behind 0% against 1%, downs 0.17 against 0.23).
- **It is not overpowered.** The best gain on any row is 6 points of clean wins over the Cytotoxic Vector (protection swarm with Ricochet); on the jobs' own fights it is 0 to 3 points. No death in any row. Ricochet is still the build that decides a swarm (+38 points over no build).
- **Hack jobs barely change.** data_pull and quiet_hours end in two or three rounds, so there is little time for a chain (swarm: 10% to 11% either way).
- **The Cytotoxic Vector is unchanged**, so the choice is real: staying power on a tough target against reach through a crowd. Today Contagion is the better pick wherever there are several enemies, and the Vector is better only against a single tough target, which no job in the sim has. If that should feel like a trade-off, the Vector needs its own edge (for example a harder tick on a two-pip enemy), which is the owner's call.
