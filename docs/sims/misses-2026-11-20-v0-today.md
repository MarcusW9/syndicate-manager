**Rules played:** climb share 1, softener +2 a miss to +6.

- **Miss:** the roll failed. **Crack:** the roll passed but a plate took it all (no HP off). **Hit:** HP came off.
- **Roll chance:** the average chance the rolls had, the bad-luck softener included (+2 a miss in a row, to +6). Today's rule: d20 + combat (+ softener) against 10 + difficulty -2 + cover (half 2, full 4).
- **Rounds with nothing landed:** rounds in which the crew fired at least once and took no HP off anyone.
