# Training focus, 2026-11-20

`node scripts/sim-focus.mjs [divert]` (about 75 seconds). Runner shaping step 3 (`drafts/PROPOSAL-runner-shaping.md`): is the nudge felt, and does the season pace hold?

Setup: a rookie (main stat 11, ceiling 18; every other stat 7 with ceiling 9, except one side stat with ceiling 14) works 22 and 60 jobs with Teo mentoring (`tests/develop-sim.js`). Infiltrator (side: combat), muscle (side: stealth), medic (side: tech); six seeds each; medians. Three ways: no focus, focus on the side stat, focus on the main stat.

| divert | jobs | focus | side | main | all stats |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.15 | 22 | none | 7 | 13 | 65 |
| 0.15 | 22 | side | 9 | 13 | 67 |
| 0.15 | 22 | main | 7 | 14 | 65 |
| 0.15 | 60 | none | 9 | 16 | 73 |
| 0.15 | 60 | side | 14 | 16 | 75 |
| 0.15 | 60 | main | 8 | 17 | 71 |
| 0.25 | 22 | side | 10 | 13 | 67 |
| 0.25 | 22 | main | 7 | 14 | 65 |
| 0.25 | 60 | side | 14 | 15 | 75 |
| 0.25 | 60 | main | 8 | 17 | 71 |

(0.25's "none" rows match 0.15's.)

**Read:** at 0.15 the focus is felt (a side stat with room +2 in a season, at its ceiling by 60 jobs) and the main stat's pace holds (13 and 16, as with no focus), so graduation and the veteran mark don't move. At 0.25 the side stat gains one more point in a season but the main stat ends a point behind by 60 jobs. Chosen: **0.15** (`FOCUS.divert`). A focus on the main stat gives +1. The total of all stats rises a little with a side focus (low stats are cheaper to raise) and falls a little with a main focus (high stats cost more).

The proposal's "about 1.5 times the share" proved the wrong measure: without a focus a side stat earns almost nothing, so any share of the points is many times what it had.
