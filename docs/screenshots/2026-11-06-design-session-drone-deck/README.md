# Design session: the drone and the cyberdeck as items (2026-11-06)

The owner asked for the drones and cyberdecks drawn in the same style as the rifle and sidearm. Three takes on each, street and pro, in the stash (sodium) and under each city light. Made with `node scripts/drone-deck-options.mjs <dir>` from `drafts/prototypes/drone-deck-options.html`. Not shipped: the owner picks first.

| Shot | What |
| --- | --- |
| 01 | Drone A, the quad: four rotors, a camera on a gimbal |
| 02 | Drone B, the kite: a small flying wing with a pusher prop |
| 03 | Drone C, the ducted fan: a drum with its fan inside and a sensor eye |
| 04 | Cyberdeck A, the slab: a keyboard with a flip-up screen and a jack cable |
| 05 | Cyberdeck B, the case: a hard-shell case, open, with an antenna |
| 06 | Cyberdeck C, the brick: a handheld with thumb keys and a whip antenna |
