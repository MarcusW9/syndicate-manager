# Design session: command fights in the Docks (2026-10-30)

`node scripts/capture-command.mjs <folder>`: a loud Warehouse Lift with four runners grown a season or so,
stopped at its first fight's orders. These are the game's own screens, not a mockup.

1. `01-orders`: the fight waiting for orders. The map is zoomed to the yard office, with faces on the tokens, a
   shield for each person's cover (or OPEN), the guards' aim with their chance to hit, and the free spots dotted.
   The panel underneath shows who the guards are aiming at, each runner's health, cover and the crew's own call,
   a target guard, and the orders with their odds.
2. `02-orders-full`: the same, as a full page.
3. `03-orders-given`: Tofu flanks guard 2 (the dotted cyan line) and Scalpel hunkers (the dashed ring). The
   chance on the line at Scalpel drops.
4. `04-after-go`, `05-after-go-full`: the next round, with the guards' new aim.
6-10: the same on desktop.
