# Design session: a fight on the watch screen, slice 6b (2026-10-29)

Taken with `node scripts/capture-fights.mjs`: a loud Warehouse Lift run with three rookies, built
with the game's own core, loaded into the page and played live by the watch screen.

Phone (390 wide): 01 the guards arrive, numbered red tokens across from the crew, a three-segment
health bar under every token; 02 a round playing (a bolt, the tally bar's "Guards 2 up"); 03 a later
round; 04 the same fight at death's door drawn as it stands; 05 its call-in, with health chips above
the choices. Desktop (1440 wide): 06-10, the same moments.
