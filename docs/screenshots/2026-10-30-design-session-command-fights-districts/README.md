# Design session: command fights in every district (2026-10-30)

`JOB=<job> PREFIX=<district>- PHONE_ONLY=1 MIN_GUARDS=2 node scripts/capture-command.mjs <folder>`: a loud run per
district with grown runners, stopped at its first fight's orders. These are the game's own screens.

- `oldtown-*`: Kidnap, at the foot of the alley: the new bins and dumpster, guards by the courtyard.
- `nightmarket-*`: Casino, at the back room: the crew behind the stalls and the new crates, the guards at the mahjong table.
- `heights-*`: Data Pull, in the loading bay: the new crates, the guards by the plaza's planters.
- `basin-*`: Wrong Horse, at the service gate: the horse trailer and hay, the guards behind the stable blocks.

Each district has five shots: 01 the orders, 02 the full page, 03 orders given (a flank or a shot, and a hunker), 04 and 05 the round after Go.
