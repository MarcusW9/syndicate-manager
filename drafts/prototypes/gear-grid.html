The squad on a grid

No tilt, no pins: everything straight and the same sizes. People in polaroids, gear in square tiles of the same width (long guns and drones turned across the square, kits and vests as they are). Signal carries a named rifle and a ghost kit, Lantern a drone, Synapse a sidearm and a vest, Drizzle (standby) a trauma kit. Each shown at phone width (390) and the plan column on a computer (920).

G · Columns

Each runner is a column: their polaroid on top, their main gear in a square tile of the same width under it, the stat under both. Photos line up with photos and guns with guns across the squad. Three to a row on a phone.

H · Roster sheet

One row per runner: the polaroid, name and stat, then everything they carry as tiles of one size, so the squad's kit reads down the page like a quartermaster's sheet. Shows the second item too (Signal's ghost kit, Synapse's vest).

I · Photo and gear side by side

Each runner is one cell holding two equal squares: the polaroid and their gear, next to each other, the stat under. Compact; four across on a computer, two on a phone.
