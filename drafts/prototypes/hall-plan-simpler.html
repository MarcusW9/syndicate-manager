What changed from the current screen

  • Two modes. The palette, the grid, the "kept clear" squares and the cover only appear while building. Looking at the hall shows the buildings and who sleeps there.
  • Three looks for parts. Ours = solid panel with a lit name, faces and OPEN ›. Next = dashed cyan, with what it gives and its price. Later = a faint outline with no name and no level. The hatching is gone.
  • Doors are gaps in the wall instead of red bars. Red now only means "do something" (the main button).
  • The way in is one dotted line from the gate to each door, so "kept clear" is a route you can follow, not scattered red squares.
  • Street furniture is quiet. Bins and carts are dim outlines while looking and filled in while building, where they matter as cover.
  • One of everything. One defences line (with a 3-step gauge), one Expand row, and Buy as a quiet text link. Only the street you come in from is named.
