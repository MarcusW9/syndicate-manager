The rest of the armoury, as items

Drawn like the rifle, the drone and the deck: a 96×32 frame, flat colour, hard light from the right, no outlines. Street is second-hand and patched; pro is clean, with the carrier's neon. Implants are the part itself, out of the body: street in a grey-market bag with a handwritten tag, pro on a clinic tray. Pick a core one for each (others can be rarer builds, as with the drone), or mix parts.

Armoured vest

Armoured vest B: The jacket

A quilted jacket with armour sewn in, collar up. Street: oxblood, a patched elbow, a faded dragon.

Armoured vest C: The weave

A slim hex-weave undershirt, high collar; worn under clothes. Street: grey, a patch over a tear.
