What this changes

  • A walled forecourt in front of every hall. It's our ground, so building there needs no excuse. The gate is the way in; the street outside stays the street. (The forecourt needs a name and a line of lore per hall, written with you through the add-lore skill.)
  • Two kinds of defence. Posts go in slots on the buildings' fronts and the gate (window post, rooftop nest, gate post, turret): that's where our people stand. Forecourt cover (barricades, plates, walls) is what the raiders have to cross. Both count towards making a raid easier.
  • Before a raid, you put your people on posts. An empty post is called out in sodium with what it costs you, so the choice is visible. Then Hold the hall starts the fight with everyone where you put them.
  • Expanding adds a second front. The Delivery Court brings a back gate onto the service lane, with its own posts. The warning says which gate they'll come through.
  • Kept as before: two modes (looking and building), one look per meaning, red only for actions, pink for the raiders (as in fights).
