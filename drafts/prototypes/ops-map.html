  • Labels are HTML over the map now, so they stay 12px (the name) and 10.5px (the line under it) at any map size. Before, they were SVG text that grew with the map: about 33px on a computer. "HEAT 0" is gone; heat shows from 1, amber from 3, red from 6 (the glow still shows it too).
  • The frame: a 1px outline a step lighter than the panel's border, cyan corner brackets, a slightly darker ground, and the legend inside it. The map reads as an object on the panel instead of melting into it.
  • Zoom: tapping a district eases the map onto it (0.45s; instant with reduced motion), dims the rest, and shows its landmark on the map. The district card beside it (under it on a phone) is the same card as now. Only taps: no pinch, no drag, so the map never scrolls under a thumb.
  • Tonight lists only what's worth a tap, and each row goes somewhere the map doesn't (in the game: the crew's card, the job board on those jobs, the rumour). Quiet districts aren't listed. Tapping a row here just shows where it would go.
