The Old Town hall, grown with Expand

A mock-up, not the game. Each Expand takes the next part of the building, and its floor is yours to build on. Level 1 is today's room; by level 4 the tong holds the whole block behind a gate. Pick a level, then tap a part of the plan (or a row under it) to zoom in to its grid. The plan is drawn small on purpose: on a phone you build zoomed in, one part at a time.

Level Screen

What changes from the game today. Expand no longer adds four squares to the same room: it takes the next part, with its own floor, and two more beds (the beds land in the part with room for them). The floor allowance goes; each part's floor is all yours. Buying the place could become "buy the building", once the whole block is held.

Inside and outside. The room, the flat and the back kitchen are inside: rooms (clinic, bunks, den ...) and beds go there. The yard is outside: defences go there, and from level 3 a raid can come through its gate as well as up the front stairs (marked WAY IN in red). The raid is still one fight, at the way in it comes through.

The order is a guess for you to change: the flat next door (level 2), the yard behind with a gate onto the back alley (level 3), then the back kitchen and the street shutter (level 4). I dropped the roof I first suggested: a plan seen from above can't show a roof over the rooms without layers.
