The squad board's background

On M (the polaroid grid scaled down), with a role icon beside each name (the picker and the runner's page still say the role) and gear names on computers only. Each at phone width (390) and the plan column on a computer (920).

N1 · Gear mat

A dark mat ruled in the inventory's cell size, so photos and gear sit on its lines; a lighter patch where each runner stands; the polaroids cast a shadow on it.

N2 · Gear mat + approach tint

The same, with the approach's colour washing in from the top and its name faint in the corner (Stealth: cool blue; Loud would be red, Con amber, Tech teal). It changes as you switch approach.

N3 · Gear mat + tint + the reading

The background reads the squad: a glow under each runner in the picker's fit colours (Lantern is shaky on this job, amber), the empty slot pulses, and the mat flashes when a change moves the risk. Tap the button under the board to see the flash.
