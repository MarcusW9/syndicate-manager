The drone and the cyberdeck, as items

Drawn like the rifle: a 96×32 frame, front to the right, flat colour, hard light from the right (the carrier's city light, or the safehouse's sodium lamp in the stash), no outlines. Street is second-hand and patched; pro is clean, with the carrier's neon. Pick one of each (or mix parts), and the rest of the armoury follows in the same hand.

Drone

Drone A: The quad

Four rotors, a camera on a gimbal. The street one: grey plastic, a taped arm, one odd orange guard.

Drone B: The kite

A small flying wing with a pusher prop, quiet and long-legged. Street: patched foam and a sticker.

Drone C: The ducted fan

A drum with its fan inside and a sensor eye; hovers in a corridor. Street: dented, a zip-tied torch.

Cyberdeck

Cyberdeck A: The slab

A thick keyboard with a flip-up screen and a cable to a neural jack. Street: odd keycaps and tape.

Cyberdeck B: The case

A hard-shell carry case, open: screen in the lid, keys and a bare board in the base, an antenna.

Cyberdeck C: The brick

A handheld: small screen, thumb keys, a whip antenna, the jack cable. Street: cracked and taped.
