The yard office, gone loud

Each round, see what every enemy is about to do, give your people their orders, then Go. Everyone starts on what they'd do on their own.

How it works
  • Shoot a guard: the odds and the damage are on the button. Armour takes 1 off a hit.
  • Cover: no shot this round, but guards aiming at you are much less likely to hit.
  • Abilities come from rank. Some need a round or two to come back.
  • Guards pick their target before you order. Half the time they go for whoever is hurt worst.
  • At 0 HP someone is down, not dead. Up to 4 rounds, then you decide: push past or pull back.
  • Enemies: a heavy charges through cover; a dog pins whoever it bites (they lose their next turn); a lieutenant makes every other guard +1 while he's up; a runner goes for the alarm and doesn't fight.
