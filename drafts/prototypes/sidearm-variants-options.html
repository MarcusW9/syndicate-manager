Sidearm looks

The rifle's kit idea for the sidearm, leaning into the neon: light strips, glowing vents, laser lines, holo sights, LED ammo counters, smart pistols with a cable to the wrist and coil pistols with glowing rings. Each gun glows in its own colour: cyan, neon purple, red or gold (a laser is always red). Standard and Asian-themed ones alongside. 7 frames × 14 finishes × 8 mods × 9 charms × 4 glow colours. In the game since 2026-10-29: two a week on the armoury's shelf, rolled at random, named, and only glowing when something on them lights up. Sidearms don't show on faces.
