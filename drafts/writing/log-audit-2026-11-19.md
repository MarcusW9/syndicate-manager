# Audit: which of the 42 action moments the log can support (2026-11-19)

Checked against `core/mission.js`, `core/incidents.js`, `core/feed.js`, `core/run.js`, `data/incidents.js`, `data/sites.js`.
Writing under audit: `drafts/writing/supplied-2026-11-19-revised.md`, section 3 (42 moments) and section 4 (the Docks pack).
Read-only audit: no code or data changed.

## What the log records today

A run's record (`am.events`, read by `feed.js` and the report) holds only:
- the **zone** (the way in, inside, the target, the way out) and the **beats** between them;
- **incident rolls** (`data/incidents.js`: lock, camera, patrol, guard, backup, checkpoint, staff, sweep, firewall, safe, hold, mark), each with the acting runner, stat, die, success or failure and its fixed cost (`fail: { alert, heat, injury, payCut, neural }`);
- **crisis rolls**, call-ins, exits, injuries, critical moments, **fight rounds** and the getaway;
- one track: **`am.alert`** (a number). Runner state: `neuralLoad` (0-100), `injury` (light/serious/critical), who is on the inside team, who waits at the van (`wheels`).

It does **not** record: a time track, a condition (fatigue) track, a clock or time of day, weather, any site feature (cameras, panels, gaps, fastenings, loads, passengers, radios), watchers, sounds or objects. `data/sites.js` plots carry approaches, fight covers and targets only. **There is no "minor check" event at all** (the small between-crisis roll the templates assume): no hook picks a template, rolls a stat and moves a track. So today **none of the 42 can be selected**; the question is what each needs once that hook exists.

## The prerequisite (not writing)

1. **A minor-check hook**: an event with actor, stat, phase, success, and the track it moved. Needs an owner decision (CLAUDE.md: no new mechanic without approval).
2. **Time and condition tracks**: 15 templates are `time` and 12 are `condition`; only the 15 `alert` ones have a track to move. Without the tracks, a failure like "has to start again" claims lost time the engine never charges. Options for the owner: (a) add the tracks; (b) run those lines as colour only, with no roll and no cost; (c) rewrite their failures to cost nothing the log can't show.

### Tier 2: needs authored tags (20)

Each needs data the game does not hold. None needs a new mechanic, but each needs an author to tag it:
- **combat 1, 2, 4, 5, 6**: obstacle / falling object / fastening / lowered load / loose panel on the site plot or in the job.
- **combat 3**: a shared heavy load and a second inside runner (the second runner is known; the load is not).
- **stealth 2, 4, 5, 6**: route alternatives, a covered fastening, a brief masking sound, a narrow gap.
- **tech 5, 6**: a casing with a warning tone; a remotely operable exit door.
- **social 2, 6**: someone to ask directions of; an observer at the way out.
- **driving 1, 2, 3, 4, 6**: a runner at the van is known (`wheels`); the constrained turn, a passing watcher, cargo, a physical transfer or passengers and uneven ground are not.
- **cool 4**: a startling sound and a casing within reach.

### Tier 3: hold out (7)

No state exists to justify them: **medical 1** (a rubbing strap, no worn-equipment data), **medical 3** (cramped hand), **medical 4** (coughing, water), **medical 6** (balance check on a second runner), **cool 2** (a timed opening), **social 4** (a real objection: only a specific crisis could supply one), **social 5** (carried load and a gatekeeper who decides pauses). The medical ones would invent symptoms from the roll, which the revision forbids.

## The Docks pack (section 4): what the log cannot gate yet

- **Slot entries** (places, obstacles, sounds, objects, watchers) carry no compatibility tags. The revision's rules ("a movable obstruction is not a locked gate", "a brief masking noise", "a catchable object") need a tag on every entry (movable / fixed / machine; brief / continuous; catchable / fixed). That is authoring work on the 126 entries, not code.
- **Time of day and weather are not in the run**, so these phase lines cannot be selected: dawn or morning (approach 2 "first shift", position 4 "sky pales", objective 4 "grey morning", exit 2 "breakfast bags", exit 3 "harbour lights begin to fade"), rain (approach 3, position 1, exit 1) and fog (approach 5, position 2). Neutral and usable: approach 1 and 4, position 3 and 5, objective 1, 2 and 5, exit 5; objective 3 (water on the deck) and exit 4 (cargo covered) need a loading job.
- **Track lines**: alert lines work off `am.alert` crossing a level (but radio, "the gap the crew used" and gate lines need the matching fact); time and condition lines have no track to cross.
- **Faction overlays**: the job's `against` (gang) is recorded; the AHL and Wet Sleeve overlays need a job-level flag saying they are in play. Check what the job data holds before relying on `against`.
- **Callbacks**: neutral fallbacks work if an earlier event has a short noun phrase. Today an event has no such phrase, so each earlier event type needs a one-line label (`{earlier}`) and a time.

## Recommendation

1. Decide the **minor-check hook** and whether to add **time and condition tracks** (the owner's call). Without them, the usable pool is the 15 alert-track templates, and only 7 of those are tier 1.
2. Do the **authoring** that costs no code: tag the Docks pack entries for compatibility; tag the Docks plot with its features (panels, gaps, a loading bay, a gantry); label incident types for `{earlier}`.
3. Keep tier 3 and the dawn/rain/fog lines out until the data exists.
