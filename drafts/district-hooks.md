# District crisis hooks (draft)

From the owner's district outline (worldbuilding prompt 3), lightly edited to the canon in DESIGN.md. Not imported by the game. Each needs choices, an abort and failure costs (WRITING_GUIDE.md) before it ships. Delete this file once they've been converted.

## Old Town
- A debt-collection run goes sideways when the target collapses with a severe neural-load seizure right outside Auntie Fung's shop.
- Brass Anchor enforcers mistake the crew for corporate arsonists scoping the To Dei Gung shrine ahead of a demolition.
- A grid-splicing job in a tenement sub-basement blacks out the whole block, just as a rival crew arrives.

## The Docks
- The container the crew came to lift is full of corporate security drones that wake the moment the seal breaks.
- A Signal 3 gale locks the dock gates automatically, trapping the crew in a restricted zone patrolled by Aether-Harbour security.
- The Ferryman won't cast off until he finishes his cigarette, smoked facing backwards, and Red Lantern enforcers use the time to surround the boat.

## The Night Market
- The client's payment chip is a Silver Fan tracker, and the debt collectors now know exactly where the crew is.
- A late-night noodle stop is caught in a data-broker clash that fries cheap implants.
- Madam Rao sends the crew to recover a ledger from a back-alley clinic; the patient on the table is a senior Harbour Police commander.

## Glass Heights
- The crew's inside contact is fired and wiped from the directory while the crew is on the 40th floor.
- A neural-enhancement trial goes wrong in the lobby and leaves the {tech} with memory stutter and corporate telemetry loops.
- A lockdown traps the crew on a sky-bridge mid-extraction, and the only way out is a rooftop climb in high wind.

## Iron Peak (locked)
- Security cuts the funicular's power for a "VIP movement", leaving the crew stuck halfway up in a glass car in full view.
- The housekeeper who sold the crew the gate code wants out, and wants to leave down the hill with them tonight.
- The estate's guard dogs are chromed, and char siu doesn't work on them.

## The Silt (locked)
- The timber walkway collapses while the crew is fleeing, dropping the cargo into the black mud.
- A generator blows at Uncle Po's yard, and the outage across the shelf blinds the crew's optical feeds.
- The crew is protecting a whistleblower in the shacks when the tide cuts off every way out and the bounty hunters arrive.

## The Amber Reach (locked)
- The crew's transit passes are revoked the moment the maglev leaves, locking the carriage doors.
- A panicked corporate courier drops a briefcase of live financial keys into the rush-hour crowd during the handover.
- Plainclothes detectives spot unlicensed hardware in the crew's bags and corner them against the ticket gates.
