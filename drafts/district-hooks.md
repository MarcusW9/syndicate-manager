# District crisis hooks (draft)

From the owner's district outline (worldbuilding prompt 3), lightly edited to the canon in WORLD.md. The four open districts' hooks shipped as `src/data/districtCrises.js` on 2026-10-25; what's left belongs to the locked districts. Not imported by the game. Each needs choices, an abort and failure costs (WRITING_GUIDE.md) before it ships. Delete this file once they've been converted.

## Iron Peak (locked)
- Security cuts the funicular's power for a "VIP movement", leaving the crew stuck halfway up in a glass car in full view.
- The housekeeper who sold the crew the gate code wants out, and wants to leave down the hill with them tonight.
- The estate's guard dogs are chromed, and char siu doesn't work on them.

## The Silt (locked)
- The timber walkway collapses while the crew is fleeing, dropping the cargo into the black mud.
- A generator blows at Uncle Po's yard, and the outage across the shelf blinds the crew's optical feeds.
- The crew is protecting a whistleblower in the shacks when the tide cuts off every way out and the bounty hunters arrive.

## The Amber Reach (locked)
- The crew's transit passes are revoked the moment the maglev leaves, locking the carriage doors.
- A panicked corporate courier drops a briefcase of live financial keys into the rush-hour crowd during the handover.
- Plainclothes detectives spot unlicensed hardware in the crew's bags and corner them against the ticket gates.

## Pearl Haven House, the Amber Reach (locked)
- "Wet Sleeve" officers raid Floor 3 for a surprise "neural-load check", shaking down the unregistered tech stalls and threatening to seal the whole wing.
- A suspicious electrical fire starts by the power risers on Floor 2, a classic Szeto buyout move, and the crew has minutes before the lower floors go up.
- An Aether-Harbour audit team follows an off-books courier into the Horizon Rest Lodge, and residents are trapped in the standoff.
