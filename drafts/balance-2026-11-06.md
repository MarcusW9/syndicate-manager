# Balance pass: Phase 2 together (2026-11-06)

Measured with `scripts/sim-phase2.mjs`: 16 seeds a year each. **Numbers only: nothing in the game was
changed.**

The sim's player runs every crew it can, hands-off (stealth, Steady, never call), on the best-paying
jobs first, and hires while cash allows. Unlike the older `scripts/sim-tong.mjs`, it answers
everything:
- **Renewals:** accepts the raise.
- **Heat notices:** lets them ride.
- **Raids:** pays off a cheap one, holds when two or more are home (fought by the real fight rules),
  and otherwise walks out.

It also opens a hall once the favour is done and the deposit plus two weeks of bills are covered.

## What the numbers say

Week 52, median of 16 runs:

| Player | Cash | Broke | Halls | Roster | Rep |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Old sim (no raises, one hall) | ¥15,430 | 25% | 1 | 12 | 83 |
| Careful, one hall only | ¥18,840 | 31% | 1 | 11 | 90 |
| **Careful, opens halls (the game as it is)** | **¥1,440** | **50%** | 2 | 12 | 91 |
| Careful, halls at half rent | ¥13,290 | 31% | 2 | 12 | 93 |
| Careful, halls with 4 beds | ¥8,510 | 38% | 2 | 12 | 84 |
| Loud, nothing built | −¥24,750 | 88% | 1 | 7 | 19 |

1. **A second hall costs more than it brings.** Opening one takes the broke share from 31% to 50% and
   median cash from about ¥18,800 to ¥1,400. The deposit (¥12,000–20,000) and the rent (¥1,000–1,600 a
   week for 2 beds) buy presence and rank, but no income: the extra jobs a week barely move (4.1 to
   4.3).
2. **Rent is the lever.** At half rent, a second hall is about cost-neutral (31% broke, as with one
   hall). More beds don't help: more beds mean more hires, and more wages.
3. **The favours work.** In 11 of 16 runs the second hall opened around week 22–26, and a favour was
   rarely failed.
4. **Raids almost never happen to a careful player:** 1 raid in 16 player-years, and no gang raids at
   all. Heat stays under 7, and grudges fade faster than six jobs a year build them. So raids, the
   defences, the turret and the gangs only surface for someone who plays loud by hand.
5. **Loud hands-off play is ruinous:** 88% broke, with reputation stuck in the teens. Loud crews on
   their own fail and abort too much. It isn't a new problem (the loud approach was never meant for
   hands-off), but it means hands-off loud isn't a path to raids.
6. **The defences couldn't be judged** here: the loud runs never had money to build. The earlier
   fight-by-fight measure stands: 6 pieces make a hall hold every raid up to heat 10.
7. **Old problem, not Phase 2:** about 30% of these greedy-hiring runs are broke by week 13 in the old
   sim too. That's the sim's player hiring fast.

## Options for the owner

**Halls**
- **a. Halve hall rent** (the Night Market ¥500, the Docks and the Basin ¥600, Glass Heights ¥800). A
  second hall becomes about cost-neutral: a choice for presence and rank, not a trap.
- **b. Give a hall income:** its contact offers one extra job a week from that district while the hall
  stands. The hall then pays through work, which is more design, and a second balance run.
- **c. Both, smaller:** rent down a third, and the extra job every other week.

**Raids**
- **a. Leave them rare:** they're there for the loud player.
- **b. A little easier to trigger:** heat 6 or more (was 7), a grudge of 5 or more (was 6), and grudges
  fading every other Sunday, not every Sunday.
